# Roadmap to a complete paper (2026-10-28)

**Sources:**
- the independent review and its addendum (`REVIEW_2026-10-28.md`);
- unscored or parked amendments (`STATUS_2026-10-27.md` section 3, `provisory_plan_paper.md` WP7–WP8);
- the PI's requests.

**Ordering principle.** Corrections that change many outputs come first:
1. decisions;
2. code;
3. data tables;
4. anchors.

Everything that reads them comes after, and the figures are regenerated once, at the end. A phase starts only when the phases it depends on are finished; tasks within a phase can run in parallel.

**Tags:**
- **[PI]:** decision needed;
- **[reg]:** amendment written and committed before scoring;
- **[sim]:** simulation only;
- **[run]:** real-data compute.

Efforts are rough estimates: wall-clock at 2 lanes of 4 workers, plus agent time.

## Phase 6. Disease: making the "rarely moves the network" claim credible (after Phases 3–5)

The reviewer's objections to Figure 4:
- nulls on classes that carry no clock signal;
- brain contrasts that depend on the anchors;
- "falls to 0" that is a truncation;
- small control groups with inconsistent baselines;
- between-person designs confounded by the circumstances of death;
- no human perturbation positive.

Order: positive controls first. They show that the readout can move in humans, which is what makes a null meaningful.
