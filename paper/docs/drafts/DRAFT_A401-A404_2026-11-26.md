# Drafts A401-A404 (2026-11-26, for the user's review; not registered)

Numbering: renumbered from A394-A397 (taken by the lead's amendments A394-A400 registered the same day). Check the
next free number in `registered_experiments.md` again before registering.

Context: the tumour's own stroma (fibroblasts, endothelium, mural cells) is used as the clock reference ("host") of each
tumour sample. A387 showed, where a normal sample exists (colorectal, lung, liver, prostate), that tumour fibroblasts and
endothelium keep the patient's normal-tissue time (coupled, within 0.8 h) and are quieter; A388 used the tumour stroma as
host for cancer cells across 14 cancer types. The drafts below extend this with public tumour datasets now being
inventoried (`exploratory/A394_cancer_timekeeping/output/feasibility/`; cap raised to 700 GB, user 2026-11-26). Each
becomes a registration, with its data list fixed, before any of its values is computed.

**Common precondition for every tumour-only cohort (P401, all drafts):** the tumour stroma is a valid host in that cancer:
(i) the anchors-only gate passes (p < 0.05); (ii) each stromal class is coupled to the host of the other stromal classes
(leave-one-out) beyond the expression-matched sets on standardised values (z of c against the matched distribution,
A389 note 2 lesson), pooled per cancer. A cancer failing P401 is uninformative for every draft (never "uncoupled").
Consistency checks reported beside it: stroma coupling within the range of the four A387 cancers; endothelium minus
fibroblast phase within the A389 tumour range. The phase of the tumour stroma is not assumed equal to the patient's in
cancers without a normal sample; drafts 2-4 need only a reference shared within the tumour.

## A401 draft: timekeeping of cancer cells across cancers, and the bulk tumour-normal pairing

**Question.** Does each cancer type's bulk tumour-normal clock pairing (A138, TCGA) follow from how much its cancer cells
and its stroma keep time, weighted by tumour composition?
**Data.** A388 table extended with the new tumour datasets; unit = patient with a malignant (or tumour-epithelium)
pseudobulk and >= 1 tumour stromal anchor (relaxed from 2; the host posterior is noisier but defined); >= 5 patients per
cancer type; deduplication as A388; P401 per cancer.
**Per cancer:** cancer-cell coupling to the tumour-stroma host c_mal (fit, 90 % intervals), excess over the matched-set
median (the shared-sample component, A388 D388.4) e_mal = max(c_mal - median_matched, 0); stroma coupling to the
patient's time c_str (A387 pooled values, per cancer where A387 has them); amplitudes A_k relative to tumour fibroblasts;
composition from TCGA: malignant share = mean ABSOLUTE purity of the cancer's TCGA primaries
(`tcga_purity_absolute/mastercalls.txt`), stroma share = 1 - purity split by the single-cell fibroblast : endothelial
ratio (immune cells carry no clock on this scale, A281).
**Predicted bulk pairing index** P = (s_mal A_mal sqrt(e_mal c_str) + sum_stroma s_k A_k sqrt(c_str)) /
sqrt(sum_k s_k^2 A_k^2) (chain model: cancer cells -> stroma -> patient; independent own noise per compartment).
**H401.1 (primary):** Spearman of P with the A138 per-cancer pairing over matched cancers (TCGA mapping fixed: colorectal
COAD, lung adenocarcinoma LUAD, liver LIHC, prostate PRAD, bladder BLCA, breast BRCA, thyroid THCA, kidney clear cell KIRC,
papillary KIRP, endometrium UCEC), > 0, exact one-sided p < 0.05. Decidable if >= 7 matched cancers (critical rho 0.71).
**H401.2 (secondary):** Spearman of e_mal with the A83 clock-shaped share across tumours (11 TCGA cancers incl. OV).
**Descriptive:** per-cancer table and figure ("timekeeping across cancers"): c_mal, e_mal, standardised z, loudness, stroma
coupling, P, bulk pairing; lung adenocarcinoma vs other lung.
**Not blind:** the five A388 D388.5 cancers' ranks (rho 0.90 core); blind: added cancers, the index P, the purity-based
composition.

## A402 draft (question 1): immune state and the tumour's internal time, powered replication

**Prior record.** A146 part 2 (213 patients, mouse phases) and A246 (any phase; omnibus T -0.12, p 0.63, MDE 0.85) found
no organisation of ten immune / tumour programs against the tumour's clock state; A229 secondaries passed (tumour T/NK
share and endothelial VCAM1 follow the tumour endothelial clock state). A146 part 1: programs follow the recorded
surgery hour in a timed breast cohort (p 0.0006), not internal time.
**Register only if** the new datasets bring the A246 design to MDE <= 0.43 (half of A246's), i.e. about 4x the
patients; otherwise closed at the power stage.
**Design.** A246 statistic and programs, state = the tumour-stroma host (full model posterior state, replacing the
endothelial z), within-cohort permutations, 200 matched pseudo-host sets (specificity) and stress-responsive pseudo-hosts
(shared-sample stress). **H402.1:** omnibus T > 0 on the new cohorts only (blind replication). **H402.2:** T/NK share
follows the host state (blind replication of A229 H229.5). Timed cohorts (recorded surgery hour), if any, reported
beside: recorded vs internal time (A146 part 1 question).

## A403 draft (question 2): tumour clock loudness and the immune state

**Question.** Is a tumour's clock louder or quieter where it is immune-infiltrated (hot) or proliferative?
**Per-tumour loudness:** log radius of the tumour-stroma host state (posterior mean |h|, as the animal / person volume of
A360 / A386), centred within cohort x chemistry; its matched pseudo-host counterpart computed identically.
**Readouts (per patient):** T/NK share and CD8 share of all tumour cells; myeloid share; an immune-hot score (mean z of
CD8A, GZMB, IFNG, CXCL9, CXCL10, PRF1 in all-cell pseudobulk); proliferation index of malignant cells (A391).
**H403.1 (primary):** loudness vs the immune-hot score: within-cohort slope pooled (cohort weights n), permutation p
(10,000, within cohort) < 0.05 two-sided, and beyond the matched pseudo-host loudness slopes (outside their central 90 %).
**H403.2 (secondary):** loudness vs malignant proliferation (A304 predicts lower loudness, one-sided). Holm over the two.
**Covariates:** cancer type and cohort by stratification; number of stromal anchors; median stromal depth (a
technical driver of radius). **Limits:** association only; dissociation stress can move both (stress-responsive
pseudo-hosts reported).

## A404 draft (question 3): immune compartments on the full model

**Question.** Do tumour macrophages, T/NK and B / plasma cells keep the tumour's (and, where a normal sample exists, the
patient's) time on the full model?
**Design.** Targets = tumour myeloid (macrophage where labelled), T/NK, B / plasma; host = tumour stroma (all cohorts) and,
in the A387 cohorts, the patient's normal stroma; coupling c, loudness and offset per class, pooled and per cancer
(>= 8 patients); companions: matched and narrow responsive sets (A271 showed the pairings fail against the responsive
family). **H404.1:** tumour macrophage (myeloid) coupling on the normal-stroma host > null and above both families'
q95 (A387 cohorts; the clean host). **H404.2:** the same on the tumour-stroma host (all cohorts; the shared-sample
component is larger here: read against both families). Lymphocytes: descriptive (A281: no readable clock in healthy
tissue). **Prior:** A227 (clock-specific pairing against matched sets), A271 (not beyond responsive), A228 (CD8-negative T).
