`STATUS_ROADMAP_2026-11-14.md`, which stays the reference for everything not repeated here. Every number was read in

## 1. Bottom line

1. **What changed since 2026-11-14.** Three lines were tested hard and the picture is now sharper. The target line
   ("what internal time controls", plan 1) ran out of public designs without a replicated headline (A259 to A262). Of
   the exceptions the biology paper was built on, two fell: the brain as a clock statement (A263) and "the fetal
   adrenal first" (A264, A266, A243, A269). One new positive appeared and was replicated blind: **the cellular
   organisation of the human clock** (A272, A274), in agreement with timed mouse tissue (A273).
2. **The stress-responsive companion is now the rule of the paper.** Results scored before it existed were rerun
   against it. What survives it is listed in section 2; what does not is in section 3.
3. **The paper's ordering is an open PI decision (Q35).** The cell-type results were scored after Q35 was written and
   are material for it: they are a positive biological result that survives both companion families and replicates,
   which the tally of Q35 did not have. Section 5 gives my reading.

## 4. What the findings say together (interpretation, not a result)

- **A person's tissues share one time, and it is carried by the structural cells.** Fibroblasts, endothelium and mural
  cells, present in every organ, hold the shared state; parenchymal cells and resident myeloid cells join it; the
  lymphocytes passing through do not. This is the organisation timed mouse tissue shows, now read in humans where
  timed sampling is impossible.
- **Lymphocytes are reached by the body's signals but do not keep its time.** They share glucocorticoid-target and
  immediate-early states with their tissue, not the clock state. Two readings are open and testable: lymphocytes have
  a weak clock, or they carry the time of where they came from.
- **Sharing is not the same as being driven.** The cells of a tissue differ by well under an hour, in mouse and human,
  so phase order gives no driver. In muscle the clock and the rhythmic program separate under shifted behaviour. What
  holds the cells together is not named (A268).
- **The clock network is robust.** Sex, age, chronic disease and a tumour next door leave it in place within about an
  hour. The exceptions that remain are blood (expected), malignant cells (cancer chapter) and brain-dead donors
  (descriptive).

## 5. Reading for the paper (the choice is the PI's, Q35)

- The headline that is both replicated and new is now two-part: **one internal time per living person, and the cells
  that carry it.** The first part alone was judged a confirmation of rodent work and of the 2023 population map; the
  second part is a human result that no timed design can give.
- It remains thinner than a tier-one biology paper needs: no mechanism, no perturbation, and the cell-type result has
  not yet been used to say anything about disease. The plan in
  `prompts/celltype_clock_program_2026-11-16.md` aims at exactly these gaps.
- The Nature Methods ordering stays available at no cost: every item of the plan is also a demonstration of the
  method (anchor-free readout, companions, calibration against timed mouse tissue).

## 6. Weak points a reviewer would find first

1. **Per-atlas power.** The replication passes pooled; on its own only one of five body atlases clears the responsive
   control for the stromal-vascular level, and each cortex cohort is below the matched q95 on its own
   (`A274.../output/per_atlas.csv`).
2. **The placebo of A274 sat near its threshold** (one of six tests at p 0.050; `placebo/tests.csv`). The
   false-positive rate of the pairwise test has not been measured over many placebo draws.
3. **Lymphocytes rest on few atlases** (two gut atlases and three optic-nerve pairs in the replication), and
   "lymphocyte" pools resident and circulating cells.
4. **Post-mortem tissue** for eye, optic nerve and cortex; the gut and kidney atlases are from living patients.
5. **Cortex offsets disagree in sign between PsychAD and SEA-AD**; no order of cortical cell classes can be claimed.
6. **The mouse calibration is one tissue**, eight mice per condition.
7. **Archive results not rerun on current code**: A91, A21 and the liver knockout controls (roadmap 1.2).
