## 1. The project in one page

**Question.** Do human organs and cell types keep circadian time with each other, rather than with the wall clock?
How does that internal organisation change when daily cues are lost, in chronic disease, and in local breaks (cancer,
psoriasis)?

**Method (`clocksync`, untimed and host-referenced).**
- **Input.** Clock-gene pseudobulks per donor × compartment: 14 clock genes, read against a conserved GTEx waveform.
- **Host state.** Across donors, compartments of the same person co-vary along the clock waveform. From that shared
  covariance a factor model estimates a latent per-donor clock state, the **host**. The host is anchored on
  compartments learned to carry it: stromal-vascular in the periphery, neurons in the CNS.
- **Readouts.** Each compartment is read against the host:
  - coupling c, the share of its clock variance that follows the host;
  - relative amplitude |z|;
  - phase offset arg z.
- **No times needed.** No sample is assigned a time, so constant offsets cancel and working-hours or hospital samples
  can be used.
- **Gate.** A model-free gate checks that the shared covariance is clock-shaped, against shuffled donor pairing and
  permuted acrophases. A failed gate is uninformative, never "decoupled".
- **Group contrasts.** These use d (Δc) and d*, coupling at a common host spread, with label permutations and a
  specificity companion.
- **Cancer coherence.** The cancer work mostly uses a different, simpler statistic: clock **coherence** across the
  tumours of a group (`decompose.components`). It measures how well each gene's leave-one-out phase matches the
  waveform, and it is not host-referenced coupling (section 5, opinion 1).

**Rules in force** (CLAUDE.md, HANDOFF, memory):
- **Pre-registration:** every hypothesis is registered and committed before scoring; anything chosen after a result is
  post hoc.
- **Numbers** come from output tables only.
- **Group comparisons** are made at a common spread (d*) or on z²/ψ.
- **Subagents:** Sonnet only, at most 2, never committing.
- **GTEx:** aggregates only.
- **Downloads:** parsed with `python -I`, never executed.
- **Git:** no remote, never push.
- **Long jobs:** through `paper/common/run.sh`.
- **Budget:** at most 16-20 workers; summed RAM caps at most about 100-120 GB; at least 20 GB free; data cap 400 GB
  (367 GB used).
- **Phase 8** (naming audit, scorecard, figure regeneration, package hardening, tutorials) is never started
  autonomously.
- **Outward-facing steps:** anything outward-facing (emails, access requests, release, licence, journal) is the PI's.
  The Q12 brain time-of-death draft must never be sent.

**Running at the time of writing:** nothing. The last job was the A163 validation, scored and committed (cac0912).

## 4. The story the data support

### 4.1 One-sentence version

From samples taken once at unknown times, the clock relations between a person's organs and cell types can be read.
They show that the body keeps a shared internal time, with the brain partly apart. That shared, clock-shaped state
persists when daily cues are lost, and chronic metabolic disease leaves organ coupling unchanged within tight bounds.
Where clocks do break, the clearest pre-specified human break is a loss of clock-gene coherence in proliferating and
ER-negative adenocarcinomas: breast, lung and stomach, but not every tumour type.

### 4.2 What the method contributes (Figure 1)

- **The real-data advantage is partial-day sampling.** Under working hours every untimed orderer fails on the GTEx organ
  map, while clocksync recovers it (A123b). Against a donor-disjoint truth this is 0.68, below the 0.7 bar, with a
  truth ceiling of about 0.83 (A123c).
- **Coupling itself is not exclusive.** Two-step orderer pipelines recover it with full-day sampling (A145).
- **Gated vs ungated.** A gated method declines to report a clock where an ungated orderer produces concordant phases
  from non-clock structure (A140, GSE266919).
- **Positive controls:** the Bmal1 KO (A129, strong, mouse), living adipose (A113) and baboon offsets (archive).
- **Calibration caveats to state:**
  - d and d* contrasts are calibrated;
  - absolute c is attenuated and its intervals undercover, so it is reported without intervals;
  - raw c contains about 0.25 of non-clock "coupling", so Δc is the clock-specific readout;
  - the gate loses sensitivity under strong shared non-clock structure.

### 4.3 Health (Figure 2)

- **Brain departs most, brain-specifically** (strong).
- **The stromal-vascular backbone is a description that depends on the anchors.** Δc shows endothelial, fibroblast and
  epithelial coupling are clock-specific; myeloid and lymphocyte coupling carry no clock information beyond pseudo
  genes.
- **"Stronger clocks are more coupled"** holds, but is largely algebraic.
- Embryo onset, digestive departure and hypothalamus are weak or Discussion only.

### 4.4 Cues lost (Figure 3)

- **Supported:**
  - a clock-shaped shared state across organs in ventilated and slow deaths that is not a GC or IEG response (strong);
  - phase relations preserved in slow deaths (moderate);
  - a shared displacement toward 0/12 h in ventilated donors (the H109.2 failure).
- **Not supported as a tested claim:** "Organs keep running clocks" (H109.2 failed) and "two modes of perturbation"
  (A132b is post hoc; A136 is unregistered).

### 4.5 Disease (Figure 4)

- **Strong negative:** "chronic metabolic disease leaves organ coupling unchanged" is now a tested equivalence for T2D
  and obesity (A164).
- **Bounded only:** for the single-cell disease atlases the honest statement is "no change detected; cohorts too small
  to exclude 0.2" (A153 has no primaries, and A165 and A167 detect nothing to stated MDEs).
- **Retired:** the archive Figure 4 forest plot on raw c, which is pre-fix and partly on immune classes without clock
  content.

### 4.6 Local breaks (Figure 5)

**Cancer is now the strongest pre-specified human positive in the project.**
- **ER− tumours are less clock-coherent:** SCAN-B and METABRIC, clock-specific, within subtype.
- **Proliferating tumours are less coherent:**
  - breast: A97 discovery, A127 replication;
  - lung adenocarcinoma: two cohorts;
  - gastric: two of three cohorts.
- **Bounds:**
  - it is not pan-cancer (A172, powered);
  - it is not seen in HCC or squamous lung;
  - it is not induced by acute ER blockade.
- **Tumour clocks still follow their own patient's normal tissue in bulk** (H138.4, strong). Malignant cells carry
  little host-coupled variance.

**Psoriasis** is a robust core-clock delay, but its specificity to the lesion state, its composition control and its
estimator robustness (A156) are open. The fibrotic-lung T-cell amplitude loss is a truncation artefact risk and goes to
Extended Data.

### 4.7 What the data do not support (claims to avoid)

- "Organs keep running clocks without cues" as a tested claim (H109.2 failed).
- "About 20 disease contrasts unchanged" on the current Figure 4 tables (pre-fix, raw c, underpowered; A153).
- "The backbone is stromal-vascular" without naming the anchors.

- "The proliferation loss is a general property of tumours" (A172).
- "Psoriasis validates coupling" (it is a phase readout).
- Absolute c with confidence intervals.

## 5. Opinion: strengths, weaknesses and possible misreadings

**1. The cancer positive does not use the method that is the paper's novelty.**
- **How coherence is computed** (`clocksync.decompose.components`): for each clock gene, its phase across the
  group's tumours is regressed on the clock state estimated from the other 13 genes. The result is compared with that
  gene's waveform acrophase. R is the amplitude-weighted resultant of these phase errors, so R = 1 means every gene
  sits where the waveform says.
- It needs no host, no gate and no anchor. A reviewer will ask why clocksync is needed for Figure 5's main result.
- **Misreading risk: R depends on signal-to-noise, just as c depends on host spread.** Lower R in a group can arise
  three ways:
  - (a) disrupted clock relations;
  - (b) intact clocks with less clock-state variance across tumours relative to non-clock variance in the clock
    genes, so leave-one-out phases become noisy;
  - (c) gene-specific regulation, e.g. cell-cycle control of PER/CRY/NR1D1 in proliferating tumours.
- The pseudo-clock companion is matched on mean and SD. It shows that arbitrary gene sets do not lose coherence as
  much. It does not separate (a) from (b) or (c), because proliferation-associated genes are excluded from the pseudo
  pool and clock genes are cell-cycle sensitive.
- The without-PER1/NR1D1 sensitivities usually strengthen the effect (GSE36376, GSE84437, STAD), so (c) is not carried
  by those two genes.
- In single-cell malignant pseudobulks, archive A87 already found off-plane variance +1.16 and per-cell synchrony
  unchanged. That is the "swamped" pattern, (b), for malignant vs normal epithelium.
- **Separating (a) from (b) needs a test of clock-plane variance.** If the clock-plane share (`log_plane` from the
  same function) falls with proliferation while off-plane variance rises, the reading is (b), "swamped". If the
  in-plane variance holds while R falls, the reading is (a), "disordered". This can be computed from the existing
  outputs and should be registered before it is read.

**2. The cancer replications are clean in design but selected.**
- The cancer types were chosen after seeing TCGA per-cancer values (LUAD, LIHC, then STAD from A172). Replication on
  independent cohorts is valid, but the claim must stay "in adenocarcinomas of breast, lung and stomach". HCC failed
  and pan-cancer failed.
- Several passes sit at the pseudo 5 % line: GSE72094 at the 5.5th percentile, GSE62254 at the 5.0th.
- "Clock-specific" is fragile for two of the six passing cohorts. The FFPE gastric cohort goes the other way.

**3. The headline method result depends on how truth is built.**
- 0.945 against an overlapping truth, 0.679 against a disjoint one.
- The comparison against the orderers survives either way (they fail on both). The absolute accuracy claim should
  carry the ceiling analysis.

**4. Coupling c is the paper's central readout and its absolute value is not calibrated.**
- It is attenuated, its intervals undercover, and about 0.25 of it is non-clock.
- This is acceptable if the paper reports ranks, Δc and d* and says so, but the abstract-level framing ("coupling
  0.8") must change.
- The anchor-free host flattens the backbone, which makes Figure 2's organising claim softer than its title.

**5. Bookkeeping that a reviewer or a scorecard will catch** (all with line numbers in appendix §13):
- the A109 and A138 Holm families were never closed;
- the H138.1 rule was changed after the power step and the verdict depends on it;
- the A142 digestive pass does not hold on the bounded statistic, with no erratum;
- the Bmal1 drop is quoted as −0.287, −0.291 or −0.293;
- A154 V154.1 has an ambiguous criterion;
- A157's registered fail rests on a boundary in the comparison design;
- the A47 sign is quoted both ways (the output table gives +0.043);
- Q13 has no recorded answer although its defaults were applied;
- `METHOD.md` is stale.

The A166-as-proliferation-replication citation is now corrected (erratum, 2026-10-31).

**6. Possibly stale tables.**
- **Figure 4 A17 contrasts** and the covariate-based gtex_cues and natural_experiments scripts were not rerun after
  default-changing changes. Nothing quoted from them should be treated as current.
- **The rest:** everything else in the main story is current or was rerun with no verdict change.

**7. The psoriasis estimator.**
- The core-cue offset failed to track a known timed shift in blood (A156) and moved with 5 gene-name mappings.
- Psoriasis has far more signal (15 series, robust to leave-one-gene-out and pseudo sets), so it is probably fine.
- But the paper's only disease phase effect rests on an estimator with a documented failure mode, and that should be
  stated.

**8. Positives worth more prominence.**
- **H138.4** is strong and directly contradicts the published claim that tumour clocks have no fixed relation to the
  body.
- **A164** turns "resilience" into a tested claim.
- **A140 and A123b** together are a clean demonstration of why gating matters.

**Linking the cancer positive to the method**, the highest value:

  - the in-plane vs off-plane variance split (opinion 1), which distinguishes "disordered" from "swamped";
  - per-gene variance inflation vs loss of covariance;
  - a leave-one-gene-out of all 14 genes in SCAN-B;

  This separates "clock disrupted" from "clock genes cell-cycle regulated" (opinion 1).
