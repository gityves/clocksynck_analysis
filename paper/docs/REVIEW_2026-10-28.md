# Independent review: "The human body keeps internal time" (clocksync), state of 2026-10-28

Read-only review by an independent Opus subagent, requested by the PI on 2026-10-28. The lead agent spot-checked the following against the files:
- the pseudo-clock pool exclusion (`_core/controls.py:12`);
- A150 `h150_1.csv`;
- the free-host family medians;
- the hand-edited anchor patch.

The review was written while the class-fix rerun was pending. Its class-fix findings (C4, C5) led to the A17 note of 2026-10-28 (anchors relearned, wider rerun). Numbers marked pre-fix will be superseded by `paper/results/logs/class_fix_compare.md`.

## Executive summary

The project has two strong, pre-registered, method-specific results:
- the GTEx organ map recovered from working-hours deaths alone, where every untimed orderer fails on the same donors (A123/A123b);
- a genetic positive control (hepatocyte Bmal1 KO) with a specificity companion (A129).

Several pre-registered "positives" are real but open to confounded readings:
- ventilated-donor coupling (A109 H109.1);
- the TCGA tumour–normal clock match (H138.4);
- the brain split (A142).

Each passes against a null that does not cover the most obvious alternative: a shared acute or glucocorticoid response, shared surgery time and batch, or block homogeneity.

The Figure 2 "stromal-vascular backbone" depends on the anchors. With the anchor-free host, parenchyma (0.67) and epithelium (0.60) sit next to fibroblasts (0.71). A150 also shows that a non-clock pseudo-clock set already reaches a mean coupling of 0.249, against 0.404 for clock genes. So "coupling" is not a clock-pure quantity, and the low-coupling immune classes carry no clock information. The Figure 4 disease nulls inherit this problem, and one primary (dcm_healthy heart:lymphocyte) sat on the mast-cell class bug.

A Cell-grade pre-specified disease positive does not exist yet in human data:
- psoriasis rests on archive post hoc discovery plus held-out replication;
- its treatment specificity failed;
- uninvolved skin is already "late".

The A129 Bmal1 KO is a clean pre-specified perturbation positive, but in mouse.

On code, these are confirmed:
- the Poisson-corrected complex correlation is unbounded and explodes at low depth;
- the pseudo-clock pool excludes the stress/GC genes, which are exactly the genes a reviewer would use as a negative control;
- tables fall back silently to legacy versions, with no rule-version key;
- the class-fix anchor edits broke the learned rule;
- the rerun scope missed outputs that the bug touched.

Nature Methods is supportable now. Cell needs a pre-registered human perturbation positive and a reframed coupling readout.

### 1.2 What a Cell reviewer will attack, and how
1. **Coupling is not clock-specific.** Pseudo-clock genes give c ≈ 0.25. Report the excess over pseudo, not raw c.
2. **The backbone is built into the anchors.** The learned rule ranks families by the same coherence that coupling measures, and the anchor-free host does not reproduce "parenchyma lower".
3. **Shared acute response.** For H109.1, H138.4 and A151, a reviewer will propose a GC or ischaemia pulse shared across the samples, and the pseudo pool removes exactly those genes (C2).
4. **No human pre-specified disease positive.** Psoriasis is a phase readout with composition confounds and failed treatment specificity.
5. **Method novelty after A145.** Two-step orderers recover coupling with full-day data, and no registered advantage survives Holm. In H145.3 the gate–coupling false-claim rate (0.127) is worse than CoFE two-step (0.06). The real-data advantage rests on A123/A140.
6. **Wording mismatches.**
   - STATUS says "22 healthy atlases (17 studies)"; `family_couplings.csv` has 24 targets, 18 studies and 14 tissues.
   - "Learned rule = stromal and vascular cells" vs 57 lymphocyte anchors in the table.
   - H109.1 is labelled "Strong" while the GC/non-temporal axis carries most of the shared covariance.
   - Muscle in A113: the registered estimator gave −0.854.

## 2. Code review

### 2.1 Confirmed findings

**C1. The Poisson-corrected complex correlation is unbounded.**
- **Where:** `clocksync/src/clocksync/stats.py:145-147`. The denominators are `E|a|² − mean(va)`, floored at 1e-12.
- **Failure:** shallow pseudobulks push values above 1. In A151 `posthoc_pooled.csv`, `share_corrected_pseudo_gt1` is 0.25 for fibroblasts, 0.31 for endothelial cells and 0.14 for epithelium, so H151.2 could not be read.
- **Exposure:** it also feeds `paired.ccorr`, H138.4, H138.5 (one cohort at 1.318) and A149.
- **Fix:** return NaN plus a flag when the denominator ≤ k·SE, or bound the value. Report the breakdown share.

**C2. The pseudo-clock pool excludes the genes that respond to a shared acute or GC pulse.**
- **Where:** `clocksync/src/clocksync/_core/controls.py:12-15`. `CLOCK_LIKE` removes TSC22D3, FKBP5, PDK4, TXNIP, SGK1, DDIT4, KLF9/15, ZBTB16, NFIL3 and BHLHE40.
- **Failure:** a shared GC pulse moves PER1 and the clock-plane projection in both samples. Expression-matched pseudo genes are mostly unresponsive, so the observed value exceeds every pseudo set and gets read as clock-specific.
- **Fix:** add a responsive-gene companion (IEG/GC targets) and PER1/PER2-free sensitivities.

**C3. `data.load` falls back silently to legacy tables, and the cache has no rule version.**
- **Where:** `paper/common/data.py:44-51`.
- **Scope:** 22 of 43 A17 datasets load legacy pickles whose class rules come from archive code and were not audited. `kidney_sc/build.py:27` has `("T", r"T cell")` case-insensitive, but no mast cells in its label list.
- **Fix:** embed a hash of the class rules in the parquet metadata and refuse a mismatch. Warn on any legacy fallback.

**C4. The hand-edited anchor patch contradicted the learned rule.** It has since been discarded, and the anchors are being relearned (A17 note, 2026-10-28).
- The learned rule selects whole families (`anchor_rule.py:95-96`).
- In ia_lung, myeloid is an anchor family, yet `ia:mast` was added as `anchor=False`.
- In the heart datasets, `heart:adipocyte` was added as False, although parenchyma is an anchor family there.
- colon/ileum T_other kept anchor=True although their content changed by up to 79 %.
- `compute_anchors.py` reproduces the frozen table exactly on the old tables (70/70).

**C5. The rerun scope was incomplete.**
- Figure 4 `a17_contrast.csv`, including the primary dcm_healthy heart:lymphocyte.
- cue_units (A85/A122): built on the fly, so stale.
- Genome twins: `_atlas/genome.py` feeds A150 and gene_rhythms.
- Tabula Sapiens: the melanocyte, T-helper and astrocyte patterns changed.

**C6. Silent drops in the family summary.**
- **Where:** `compute_family_summary.py:62`, `:66` and `:73`. A gate failure, a vanished anchor or an exception returns empty with only a print.
- **Fix:** log a reason row per dropped target, and assert the expected counts.

**C7. An anchor's amplitude and offset are read on a host that contains the anchor.**
- **Where:** `clocksync/src/clocksync/dataset.py:323` (`z0 = on_host(d, k, full)`). This is documented in METHOD §9.
- **Failure:** this biases |z| for anchors relative to non-anchors.
- **Fix:** use the leave-one-out host, or quantify the bias by simulation.

**C8. Bootstrap failures are dropped silently.**
- **Where:** `dataset.py:331` and `_core/host.py:268`.
- **Failure:** the intervals are conditional on non-failing replicates, and failures likely concentrate in small groups.

**C9. A123 truth (i) overlaps the test donors** (`registered_experiments.md:374`).

**C10. Public `suggest_anchors` includes lymphatic endothelium.**
- **Where:** `dataset.py:41`.
- **Problem:** the colon validation showed that lymphatic endothelium decouples.

### 2.2 Suspicions (not confirmed)

**S1. Gate false-pass rate under shared non-clock structure.** In A145 arm N the gate passed 4 of 30 runs (13 %). The acrophase-permutation null tested in isolation with a rank-1 shared factor was calibrated (0.040 and 0.051). The A145 excess is therefore chance (binomial p ≈ 0.06), or it comes from the simulator's factor structure or from depth/Poisson effects. A registered calibration study is needed.

**S2. The ILD lung-T headline may be an artefact of a small control group.** Natri controls (c 0.452, n 18) vs HLCA controls (0.046, n 30; 0.140, n 97).

**S3. `boundary` conflates two situations** (`host.py:219`).
- The flag is set for psi_ml ≤ 1e-7.
- 14 boundary groups read c = 0 because z2 was truncated, which means pure noise, not c near 1.
- 52 "ok" rows contain a group with c exactly 0.

**S4. Pooling uses asymptotic DerSimonian–Laird** (anchor learning; H138.1). This is anti-conservative with few studies; Hartung–Knapp would be safer. `stats.meta` silently drops SE 0 or NaN.

**S5. Gate seeds depend on registry row order** (`compute_family_summary.py`, `seed=i`). Adding a target changes every gate's permutations.

### 2.3 Registered design vs code
- **H151.2:** the registered statistic broke down (C1). The bounded post hoc statistic is correctly labelled post hoc. It should not appear in the main text without a fresh registration on new cohorts.
- **H138.1:** the decision rule was recalibrated after power and before scoring. Documented and acceptable.
- **A147:** the reference size was unspecified at registration. Correctly flagged.

### 2.4 Package readiness
- **Tests:** 29 pass in 12.8 s. They are smoke and recovery tests; none checks null calibration.
- **Tutorials:** one (`tutorials/01_quickstart.ipynb`).
- **Defaults:** API defaults are below the paper settings (METHOD §9).
- **Code living outside the package:** anchor learning and the identity diagnostics are still in `paper/`.
- **Docstrings:** they cite internal amendment numbers.
