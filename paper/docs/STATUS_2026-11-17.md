# The organisation of the clock in the human body: state of the evidence (2026-11-17)

Supersedes the findings sections of `STATUS_2026-11-16.md` (its weak points and housekeeping still apply where not
updated here). Every number was read in the output table named in the results entry (RP, `results_preliminary.md`)
cited next to it. Literature is from memory and is to be verified before it enters another document. Post hoc
statements are marked.

## 1. Global message

The body keeps **one internal time per person**. The **living brain** holds the organs in a fixed arrangement, whether
or not the body is aligned to the external day. Each organ holds that time with its own **self-sustained clock**, at a
phase set by how the brain's outputs reach it and with a small, clock-specific **departure of its own**. It expresses
the time with a **gain** that depends on its resident structural cells, each cell class contributing a fixed share
wherever it sits. **Circulating immune cells take no part.** Tissues reached by additional routes deviate (direct
nerves: pineal, probably the adrenal; environmental exposure for frontier tissues is post hoc and not confirmed).

## 2. The levels of the organisation

| level | what it is | set by | evidence (RP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alignment to the day | where the whole system sits in external time | external cues, behaviour | lost in slow deaths (A109c) |
| Organ arrangement | each organ's fixed phase relative to the others | the living brain, not daily alignment or behavioural cues | kept in slow deaths 0.784 and ventilated living-brain donors 0.690, above all 200 matched and 200 responsive sets; brain-dead donors 0.161 (descriptive) (A293) |
| Organ clock | a self-sustained oscillator with its own departure | the oscillator | removal of the feeding rhythm keeps 0.72 of the liver amplitude (additive cues predict 0.15) and moves it 1.6 h; inversion moves it 11.7 h (A297); own variation clock-specific, 2.6 times matched genes in every tissue, weak scaling with the rhythm (slope 0.22) (A294b) |
| Organ phase | where each organ sits | the organ's inputs, not its composition | composition moves phase within 0.13 h per SD (A295); cell types of one organ within 0.6 h (A272-A274); adrenal offset +4.4 h is a whole-clock property (A296) |
| Organ gain | how loudly an organ expresses the shared time | composition (more stromal-vascular content, higher gain: +0.041 per SD, predicted before A295, reported not tested) and probably an organ-level factor (section 3) | A293, A295 |
| Cell participation | which cells carry the time, with which relative gain | cell class, the same in every organ | stromal-vascular over lymphocyte in 3 independent sources and in living tissue (A272-A283); lymphocytes no readable clock (A281, A287); relative gains conserved, tau 0.10 to 0.31 (A293) |

## 3. Open points with their current evidence

- **Is gain fully composition?** Probably not (argument, not a test): clocked cell classes span a factor 1.9 of gain
  (A293), GTEx tissues 3.1 without blood and testis, and the same class differs 1.3 to 2.7 times between organs
  (A291 post hoc). The direct test (A298) was closed before scoring: deconvolution of GTEx was unreliable.
- **Amplitude-coupling relation.** Explained: coupling = shared rhythm / (shared rhythm + own variation); the shared
  rhythm spans 85-fold, the clock-specific own variation about 4-fold (A291, A294b). Not a measurement artefact (ceiling
  0.97). Calibration against the responsive family (A290) was interrupted and is not scored.
- **Adrenal.** Offset +4.4 h on the core clock (A296). Baboon: medulla +5.9 h, cortex +0.9 h. In GTEx, medulla
  markers do not move a sample's phase (A299 FAIL); cortex content does, towards the body's time (-1.15 h per SD,
  descriptive): the non-cortex part of a sample is advanced, its identity not shown. With the fetal adrenal (cortex
  covariation carried by ACTH-induced clock genes, medulla none; A244, A264, A266), a two-route reading (cortex
  hormonal, the advanced part neural) is a hypothesis.
- **Frontier tissues** (lung, skin, gut mucosa): lower coupling (0.51 against 0.69), more own variation, more weight on
  the hour of death (post hoc, list chosen after seeing the table); no season effect (A300 FAIL).
- **Pineal:** coupling 0 in baboon and sheep (A289), consistent with direct sympathetic drive (post hoc).

## 4. Known, expected, new (literature from memory)

- **Known (rodents):** a brain master clock synchronises self-sustained peripheral clocks; feeding entrains the liver;
  SCN lesions desynchronise peripheral tissues; organ phase maps (mouse, baboon) and the human population map
  (Talamanca 2023).
- **Expected:** bulk tissue is a sum of its cells; composition modulates amplitude; constant offsets at a shared steady
  state.
- **New in humans:** one shared, clock-specific state per living person that advances with elapsed hours (A251-A256);
  which cells carry it (structural cells and macrophages), tissue lymphocytes without a readable clock, and relative
  gains conserved across organs; the organ arrangement kept without alignment to the day and without behavioural cues,
  lost with brain death (descriptive); the amplitude-coupling relation as gain over a constant clock-specific
  departure.

## 5. Not answerable with public data

The SCN itself; the organ-level drive of gain (needs single-cell data of several organs of the same donors or a
calibrated deconvolution); the identity of the systemic signals (A268 negative); human perturbations; cortex cell-class
order (needs time of death, Q37).
