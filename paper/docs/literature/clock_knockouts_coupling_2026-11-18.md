## Verdict
- Direct "one cell type clockless, measure the others" data are almost absent. Best case: Guan 2020 (hepatocyte Rev-erb dKO): rhythmic transcriptomes/metabolomes of other liver cell types change, so non-hepatocyte readouts depend on the hepatocyte clock (a drive/coupling effect, but Rev-erb loss also alters feeding-linked/metabolic signals; not a clean Bmal1 test).
- Kornmann 2007: with hepatocyte clock arrested, liver explants lose Per2::luc rhythms; in vivo only 31 genes stay rhythmic (systemic cues). Hepatocytes are the dominant oscillator of the liver bulk signal.
- Sinturel 2021: liver rhythms persist without SCN, or with oscillators only in hepatocytes; hepatocytes hold phase coherence autonomously.
- Coupling: Finger 2021 reports paracrine TGF-b coupling of peripheral oscillators (mostly U-2 OS and explants); Welsh 2004 saw no coupling between dissociated fibroblasts; Liu 2007 SCN coupling as reference. Evidence is mixed and culture-dependent.
- Not found: mosaic/chimeric Bmal1-null plus wild-type peripheral tissues, or co-culture of clock-dead and reporter cells, with rhythm of the remaining cells measured. No cell-type-resolved (scRNA) readout of non-targeted cells after cell-type KO beyond Guan 2020 (bulk-sorted/multi-omic; verify methods).
- Most cell-type KO papers (adipocyte, beta cell, cardiomyocyte, club cell, muscle) report physiology and target-cell readouts only; they do not test pacing.
- Caveat: Welz 2019 correct DOI is 10.1016/j.cell.2019.05.009 (07.030 is a different Cell item, volume 178 p.1029, same title).
