# Animal data with loss of the central clock and several organs (scout, 2026-11-18)

Sonnet subagent scout (metadata and web pages only; eLife / PMC partly blocked); DOIs verified on Crossref except Sinturel (bibliographic match). Context: A317 / A318 (GTEx brain death).

DOIs verified on Crossref unless marked. No raw-data candidate with dense multi-organ time series from the same SCN-less animals was found; the best are luminometry/qPCR designs.

## Ranked shortlist
1. Izumo et al. 2014 eLife, DOI 10.7554/eLife.04617 (verified). Forebrain Bmal1 KO (Nes-Cre/Six3-type, SCN clock lost) x PER2::LUC. Eight tissues (SCN, DMH, pituitary, liver, kidney, heart, lung, spleen) harvested from the same mice, ex vivo luminometry; LD, DD >30 d, DD + restricted feeding. n=15 per genotype (LD, DD), 11 (DD+FR). Single harvest time per condition, so phase = ex vivo peak phase per organ per animal -> between-organ phase relations per animal directly testable (intact vs KO), plus amplitude. No GEO accession seen; source data files on eLife (Fig 5, 8, 9). VERDICT: usable for between-organ phase test (>=2 organs per animal); best fit. Need to extract per-animal peak phases/amplitudes from source data.
2. Yoo et al. 2004 PNAS, DOI 10.1073/pnas.0308709101 (verified). SCNx and sham mPer2Luc mice (Fig 6: n=8 control, n=11 lesioned), several tissues (liver, kidney, etc.) ex vivo luminometry; main claim: peripheral rhythms persist, phase desynchrony among tissues within and between animals. No accession; per-animal data only in figures/supplement. VERDICT: usable for between-organ phase test in principle (original SCNx; digitisation needed).
3. Husse et al. 2014 FASEB J, DOI 10.1096/fj.14-256594 (verified). Syt10-Cre Bmal1 fl/fl (SCN clock ablated); peripheral clock gene rhythms stay synchronised in LD, dampen then desynchronise in DD. Tissues: liver and others by qPCR/luciferase (not confirmed from text read; check full text). No accession found. VERDICT: probably usable per organ, between-organ only if same animals reported; check full text.
4. Sinturel et al. 2021 Genes Dev, DOI 10.1101/gad.346460.120 (Crossref match). SCNx vs sham Per2::luc whole-body bioluminescence in freely moving mice (DD) plus hepatocyte-only clock; shows SCN needed for between-oscillator synchrony. Single organ-resolved? No: whole-body signal, liver-focused. No accession. VERDICT: usable only per organ / whole-body; not for between-organ phase.
5. GEO GSE296764 (2026-05-08, no publication listed): SCN, liver, heart after central VIP-Cre Bmal1 KO, CT0 and CT12 only, mouse RNA-seq. Same animals not stated. Two timepoints: amplitude contrast possible, phase not. VERDICT: usable only per organ (amplitude); worth checking sample table for animal pairing.

## Others
- Tahara et al. 2012 Curr Biol, DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2012.04.009 (verified): in vivo PER2::LUC imaging of liver, kidney, submandibular gland in SCNx mice, same animals, ~1 day imaging windows; SCNx lowers amplitude, period unchanged. Different organs in same animals, phase from images; no accession. Usable in principle for between-organ phase (digitise); weak.
- Smith et al. 2023 Cell Rep, DOI 10.1016/j.celrep.2023.112588 (verified): Bmal1 reconstitution liver/muscle/both; liver + gastrocnemius RNA-seq every 4 h, n=3, GSE197455, GSE197726, GSE228389. Not an SCN-loss design (clockless background, organ-level rescue) and tissues likely from separate animals per timepoint. Usable per organ; liver-muscle phase relation across reconstitution arms is a distinct question.
- Koronowski 2019 / Greco 2021 / Welz 2019: liver (GSE132659) and hepatocyte (GSE171183), muscle (GSE171432) Bmal1 rescue/KO datasets; single organ per study -> not usable for between-organ.
- Yeung et al. 2018 Genome Res, DOI 10.1101/gr.222430.117 (verified; 2017 online): Bmal1 KO vs WT liver and kidney temporal RNA-seq (clockless, not SCN loss), systemic signals keep low-amplitude rhythms. Two organs but not central-clock-specific; usable per organ.
- Constant light: GSE226855 (liver, hypothalamus, fat, gut segments, spleen, mammary, thymus; DD/LD/LL conditions) -- multi-organ, but central clock not removed, likely separate animals per timepoint; accession from search snippet, not verified here. Food-entrainment multi-omics (fat, liver, kidney, heart; LL factor) -- accession not found.
- Vgat-Cre Bmal1 chimeric mice (UMass repository paper, DD and shifts): chronic peripheral amplitude loss, bibliographic details not retrieved. Nms-Cre: nothing found.
- Brain-death animal models: only rat renal/liver transplant-donor expression studies (e.g. Schuurs 2004 Am J Transplant, DOI 10.1111/j.1600-6143.2004.00607.x), no clock-gene multi-organ time series. Not usable.

## Gaps
No public RNA-seq with several organs per SCN-lesioned/central-KO animal and multiple timepoints was found. The realistic route is per-animal ex vivo PER2::LUC phases (Izumo, Yoo) or contacting authors. Izumo and Husse accession/data availability should be confirmed from full text (eLife page blocked in this session; PMC fetch partial).
