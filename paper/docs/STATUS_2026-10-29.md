## 1. Decision needed: Q15 (A153 parked)

A153 is the corrected disease contrast for every Figure 4 contrast. As registered, it has no primaries, for two
reasons:
- No disease control arm has a genome-wide table on disk.
- The per-compartment "clock-specific" flag from A159 is underpowered. A single compartment's pseudo-gene q95 is 0.6-0.9,
  so 3 of 31 healthy compartments pass, all in breast; heart fibroblast at c 0.72 fails.

I recommend **(a)**: eligibility by family, pooled over the 5 healthy atlases with genome tables. (b) is genome tables
for about 18 disease contrasts, 1-2 days of work. (c) is no eligibility rule. Changing the rule now is a design change
after a result, so I wait for you.

## 2. New results

**Strong**
- **A127 (SCAN-B, 3,073 breast tumours).** Tumour clock coherence falls with proliferation within PAM50 × purity:
  T −0.123, Holm 0.0045, clock-specific at the 2.5th pseudo percentile. ER− tumours are less coherent: −0.343, Holm
  0.001, 1.5th percentile. Purity runs the other way (low purity is more coherent). There is no survival association.
  This replicates A97 at scale and is a candidate Figure 5 panel.

**Moderate / restated**
- **Phase 5 bounded rescoring** (C1 fix; descriptive or post hoc; no registered verdict changed):
  - "Brain departs most" stands: r* 0.639 vs q05 0.936, and the same on the uncorrected r.
  - The digestive departure does not hold on r.
  - The hypothalamus is 10th of 13 in fast deaths.
  - A149: H149.1 (0.490, p 0.011) and H149.3 (0.707, p 0.0004) hold, but the pairing is still not clock-specific.
  - Lung carries the per-cancer pairing (0.595, p 0.007).
  - H149.2, H151.2, the endothelial pseudo companion and H138.5 are **not estimable** on the bounded statistic.
  - A134: the permutation p (0.017) disagrees with the patient bootstrap (CI [−0.16, 0.27]).
- **Phase 3 reruns on the relearned anchors:** no registered verdict changed. In the family ranking, stroma, vascular
  and neurons stay above myeloid and lymphocytes under the learned, fixed and free hosts.
- **A159 (Δc):** mean coupling 0.387 vs matched pseudo 0.227; median Δc endothelial 0.31, fibroblast 0.23, lymphocyte
  0.02. Breast plasma cells are clock-specific (c 0.81), which is unexpected.

**Failed or not testable**
- **A140 CYCLOPS with the core-clock seed:** the gate fails (median 0.058, 0/50), and at scale 0 CYCLOPS never starts
  (0/20). So the straw-man objection is answered: CYCLOPS does not recover the clock at this design even when seeded
  with clock genes.
- **A160 (keratinocyte-only psoriatic delay, GSE173706):** not testable. With 10 pairs the MDE is 5.4 h (bins 3.9 h)
  against a 1.7 h delay. Descriptive −2.8 h, which depends on NR1D1.
- **A126 (menstrual cycle):** all three tests fail.
  - H126.0, the endometrial proliferation gate: −0.141, p 0.10.
  - H126.1, breast luteal tertile: +0.029, p 0.58, MDE 0.34.
  - H126.3, obesity.

  A large descriptive secretory-phase drop in endometrial epithelial coherence (0.653 → 0.126) did **not replicate** in
  three bulk series (A161: T +0.096, opposite sign, MDE 0.117).
- **4.6 (Bmal1 drop gap, simulation):** the clocksync drop (−0.29) exceeds the timed oracle's (−0.19). Only "noise loss
  plus the KO liver no longer following the mouse's own deviation" reproduces the ratio. The gap is therefore
  compatible with host- vs ZT-referenced estimands, not an artefact of spread (d* −0.287).

## 3. What this means for the paper

- **Cancer (Figure 5):** the proliferation and ER coherence loss is now strong (A97 + A127). The tumour-normal pairing
  is weaker than before: lung only, not clock-specific, several readouts not estimable. Lead with coherence vs
  proliferation; give the pairing as descriptive.

  works" panel.
- **Psoriasis:** the bulk delay is robust (2026-10-28), but composition control cannot be done with current
  single-cell data.
