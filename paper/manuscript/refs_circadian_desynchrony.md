## Close to our gap (synchrony or coherence between tissues or cells within individuals)

No study measures phase offset, coupling or relative amplitude between tissues within the same human, untimed, in a population. Closest:
- **hughey2016differential**: pools public human datasets; brain clock phase ~8.5 h offset from blood. Across individuals, not within; authors state within-person phase relations of peripheral clocks unknown.
- **ruben2018database / talamanca2023sexdimorphic**: tissue-specific rhythmic gene sets and CHIRAL phase inference over 46 GTEx tissues; tissue phases are compared at the population level (time of death), not as per-donor synchrony.
- **wefers2018circadian, skene2018separation, kervezee2018simulated, kervezee2019individual**: human misalignment protocols that read out two compartments (muscle vs blood/plasma metabolites; PBMC) in the same volunteers, with small n and sampled across timed protocols.
- **archer2014mistimed, moller-levet2013effects**: blood transcriptome under forced desynchrony: loss of rhythmicity within one compartment, not between organs.
- **yamazaki2000resetting, tahara2012in, damiola2000restricted, welz2019bmaldriven, koronowski2019defining, sinturel2021circadian, heyde2022induction, kolbe2019circadian**: mouse/rat, inter-tissue or tissue-SCN phase relations measured by reporters or tissue-specific rescue; the experimental precedent for uncoupling.
- **galinde2023different**: review defining levels of desynchrony and stating the lack of human biomarkers for it.
- **Within tissue, single cell**: **hollis2026singlecell** (ORPHEUS, Anafi lab; cell-to-cell variance as intercellular desynchrony; loss in AD excitatory neurons, human brain), **salati2026inferring** (scRitmo, Naef lab), **veltri2025singlecell** (mouse liver scRNA-seq, aligned clock across cell populations), **nagoshi2004circadian / welsh2004bioluminescence** (single fibroblasts, independently phased), **delaiglesia2004forced** (desynchrony within SCN), **reddy2002differential** (SCN subregions). Reviewers should see that ORPHEUS (cells within a tissue) and our work (compartments within a person) are complementary.
