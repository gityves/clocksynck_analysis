# saini2013realtime
**Ref:** Saini et al., Genes Dev 2013 (27:1526-1536). DOI 10.1101/gad.221374.113. Type: method / biology (RT-Biolumicorder). Lab: Schibler (with Franken).
**Source:** full text

## Question and design
Real-time recording of hepatocyte luciferase reporters (adenoviral Bmal1-luc, Rev-erba-luc; Per2::Luc) in freely moving
mice; feeding inversion (night to day feeding) in sham vs SCN-lesioned mice (n = 3 each). **Sampling:** continuous,
1-min photon counts over days.

## Method (if any)
Filtered traces; phase of peaks per cycle; phase angle change after feeding inversion.

## Key results
- "hepatocyte oscillators of SCN-lesioned mice synchronized more rapidly to feeding cycles than hepatocyte clocks of intact mice" (Abstract): new phase within 1-2 days without the SCN; intact mice advanced only about 6-7 h after 7 days (Fig 5).
- Conflict: "the SCN uses signaling pathways that counteract those of feeding rhythms when their phase is in conflict with its own phase." (Abstract)
- The SCN also sets part of the steady-state liver phase beyond feeding: SCNx livers were somewhat delayed already under night feeding.
- SCNx ad libitum: liver rhythms persist for several days, with lower amplitude (possible partial dephasing of hepatocytes; caveat that surgery or injection may reset).

## Not addressed / limits
- n = 3 per arm; liver only; adenoviral transduction varies between mice.

## Link to us
- **Intro claim(s):** organs integrate competing drives: central signals and feeding; the central drive's weight sets how far feeding can move an organ.
- **Relation to clocksync:** supports the conflicting-drives part of the model (weight b, saturating response; A257 night shift, gap 5).

## Ideas
- The shift kinetics with and without SCN give a mouse calibration of b for liver; compare with Manella 2021 (liver vs lung vs fat).
