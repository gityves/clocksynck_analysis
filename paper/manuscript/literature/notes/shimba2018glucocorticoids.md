# shimba2018glucocorticoids
**Ref:** Shimba A, ..., Ikuta K, Immunity 48:286-298 (2018). PMID 29396162. Type: biology (mouse). Lab: Ikuta (Kyoto).
**Source:** abstract only (web search on 2026-11-12; no verbatim quotes). Not in the Zotero export.

## Question and design
Do glucocorticoids drive the daily redistribution of T cells? Mice, including T-cell-specific glucocorticoid receptor deficiency. **Sampling:** timed.

## Key results (paraphrased from the abstract)
- Glucocorticoid receptor signalling induces IL-7R in T cells with a daily rhythm; IL-7R supports survival and, through CXCR4, the redistribution of T cells between lymph nodes, spleen and blood.
- T-cell accumulation in the spleen at night enhances responses to soluble antigen and systemic bacterial infection.
