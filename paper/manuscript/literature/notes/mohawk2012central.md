# mohawk2012central
**Ref:** Mohawk, Green & Takahashi, Annu Rev Neurosci 2012 (35:445-462). doi:10.1146/annurev-neuro-060909-153128. Type: review. Lab: Takahashi.
**Source:** full text

## Question and design
Review of central (SCN) and peripheral oscillators: SCN network coupling, peripheral clock entrainment (autonomic, glucocorticoid,
temperature, feeding), clocks and metabolism. Rodent studies. **Sampling:** not applicable; synthesis of timed rodent studies.

## Method (if any)
None.

## Key results
- SCN: about 20,000 neurons; "SCN neurons exhibit a wide range in cell-autonomous circadian periods that vary from 22 h to 30 h", pulled by coupling to a narrow range (SD of the locomotor period about 0.2 h, i.e. 12 min).
- Coupling provides robustness: Cry1-/- SCN slices oscillate in a synchronised way while dispersed cells are arrhythmic (Fig. 2); in Bmal1-/- SCN, network coupling still propagates stochastic oscillations.
- Peripheral clocks are autonomous but need the SCN in vivo: "loss of the SCN results in peripheral circadian clocks that become desynchronized".
- Tissue specificity: "3-10% of all mRNAs in a given tissue showing circadian rhythms" and "the genes that are under circadian control are largely nonoverlapping in each tissue".
- Liver-specific clock disruption (Kornmann 2007): most hepatic transcripts lose rhythm but a subset including Per2 continues to cycle, so rhythms need both local clocks and systemic cues.
- Entrainment pathways (Fig. 3): autonomic innervation, glucocorticoids, temperature (about 2.5 C body-temperature oscillation), feeding and local cues.
- Desynchrony between peripheral clocks: "In SCN-intact animals, phase desynchrony among peripheral oscillators can occur"; methamphetamine "results in desynchrony among internal oscillators".

## Not addressed / limits
- No human or cancer data; "desynchrony" is described qualitatively from rodent perturbations (feeding, drugs, SCN lesion) with no effect sizes in hours.
- Coupling is discussed within the SCN network, not between organs; whether peripheral tissues couple to each other is left open.
