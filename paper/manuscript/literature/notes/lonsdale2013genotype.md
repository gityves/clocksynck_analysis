# lonsdale2013genotype
**Ref:** Lonsdale J et al. (GTEx Consortium), Nat Genet 45:580-585, 2013. DOI 10.1038/ng.2653. Type: data (project description, pilot). Lab: GTEx Consortium.
**Source:** full text (Commentary; Supplementary tables not included)

## Question and design
Description of the GTEx pilot: a rapid-autopsy and organ-donor programme to build a biobank of reference tissues for eQTL studies. No results from donors; power calculations and procedures only. **Sampling:** post-mortem, within 24 h of death; clock time of collection and time of death are not mentioned.

## Method (if any)
Not applicable. Describes caHUB workflows: Biospecimen Source Sites collect aliquots, PAXgene fixation, shipping to a central repository, pathology review, RNA extraction.

## Cohort and collection (what the text says)
- Pilot 2.5 years, then "scaling up to include approximately 900 postmortem donors by the end of 2015" (pilot goal).
- Sources: "GTEx donors are identified through lowPMI (post-mortem-interval) autopsy or organ and tissue transplantation settings." A small surgical set (skin, fat, muscle) is also collected for comparison.
- Eligibility: ages 21-70, and "biospecimen collection can start within 24 h of death". Exclusions: HIV, hepatitis, metastatic cancer, chemotherapy or radiation within 2 years, transfusion in past 48 h, BMI > 35 or < 18.5. The cohort is therefore not disease-free: "donor eligibility is broad and is not restricted to specific diseases or conditions".
- Ventilator: brain is collected from "deceased donors who were not on a ventilator for the 24 h before death" (only a criterion for brain donation; no ventilator cases counts given).
- Annotations: donor-level data "include basic demographics, medication use, medical history, laboratory test results and the circumstances surrounding" the death, obtained from next of kin for post-mortem donors; sample-level data "include the tissue type, ischemic time, comments from the prosector and pathology reviewer".
- Power: "At an effective sample size of 750, we would have 80% power to detect cis eQTLs with MAF as low as 2%".
- The Hardy scale is not mentioned.

## Not addressed / limits
- No timing information relative to the clock: no time of death, no collection hour, no day/night comparison. No ischaemic times given as numbers; no counts of ventilator, fast or slow deaths.
- A 2013 commentary with plan and design, not data; donor numbers are targets.
