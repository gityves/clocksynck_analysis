# gekakis1998role
**Ref:** Gekakis et al., Science 1998 (280:1564). Type: biology. Lab: Weitz (with Takahashi).
**Source:** full text (the extracted file begins with the tail of an unrelated physics paper; the Gekakis article follows and is complete).

## Question and design
Mouse/hamster molecular biology: yeast two-hybrid screen of a hamster hypothalamus cDNA library with CLOCK as bait, in situ
hybridisation (mouse SCN and retina), yeast one-hybrid DNA binding, luciferase reporters in mammalian cells (mper1 and
Drosophila per E-boxes). **Sampling:** not a time series; a single expression snapshot per probe, no circadian time coverage.
In vitro and cell-line assays (>=3 or >=6 independent experiments per panel).

## Method (if any)
None (mechanistic experiments).

## Key results
- Screen of 2x10^7 yeast transformants gave 154 double-positive colonies; BMAL1 was the strongest bHLH-PAS partner. "we ultimately identified 22 distinct CIPs that gave two-hybrid signals specific for CLOCK" (Results, Fig. 1).
- bmal1 transcripts coexpress with Clock and mper1 throughout the rostrocaudal SCN and in retina (Fig. 2), placing BMAL1 in clock cells.
- Only the CLOCK-BMAL1 pair, not CLOCK-ARNT or CLOCK-ARNT2, bound the per E-box in the one-hybrid assay (Fig. 3).
- The mper1 5' flank (3.2 kb sequenced) carries three E-boxes within 1.2 kb of the start site (Fig. 4A).
- "CLOCK and BMAL1 together, but not either alone, produced a substantial increase in transcriptional activity" (9.3-fold, P < 0.005, 2.0-kb fragment; Fig. 4B).
- Three E-boxes linked: "but CLOCK and BMAL1 together produced a substantial transcriptional activation" (14.1-fold, P < 0.0005, Fig. 4C); individual E-boxes 2.1-, 3.6-, 8.5-fold (distal, middle, proximal).
- Dominant-negative Clock allele: "CLOCK-D19 and BMAL1 together showed no transactivation activity from mper1 E-boxes" yet "Thus, CLOCK-D19 heterodimerizes, apparently normally, with BMAL1 and other bHLH-PAS proteins" (Fig. 5).

## Not addressed / limits
- Heterologous (yeast, COS/NIH3T3-type) assays: no endogenous rhythm is measured, so the paper shows activation, not oscillation.
- Negative limb is only proposed (PER-dependent inhibition), not tested; CRY is absent.
- No tissue other than SCN and retina; no statement about peripheral organs, cell types or humans.
