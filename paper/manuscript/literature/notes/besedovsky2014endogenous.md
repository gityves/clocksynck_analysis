# besedovsky2014endogenous
**Ref:** Besedovsky L, Born J, Lange T, FASEB J 28:67-75 (2014). DOI 10.1096/fj.13-237958. Type: biology (human). Lab: Born / Lange.
**Source:** abstract only (bibliographic records found by web search on 2026-11-12; no verbatim quotes). Not in the Zotero export.

## Question and design
Does endogenous cortisol, through the glucocorticoid receptor, drive the morning changes of human T-cell subsets? Two placebo-controlled studies: mifepristone (receptor antagonist, given at 23:00 h) and metyrapone (cortisol synthesis blocker, given at 04:00 h); CXCR4 expression and T-cell subset counts by flow cytometry. **Sampling:** timed, night to morning.

## Key results (paraphrased from the abstract)
- Metyrapone blocks the morning rise of CXCR4 on naive T cells; mifepristone attenuates it.
- Both blunt the morning fall of naive T-cell numbers (less clearly with mifepristone, a partial agonist at night).
