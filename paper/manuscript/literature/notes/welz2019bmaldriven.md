# welz2019bmaldriven
**Ref:** Welz et al., Cell 2019 (177:1436-1447). DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2019.05.009. Type: biology (tissue-specific reconstitution). Lab: Aznar Benitah / Sassone-Corsi.
**Source:** full text

## Question and design
Do tissue clocks respond to light independently of each other? Bmal1-stopFL KO crossed with K14-Cre gives epidermis-only Bmal1 (RE); WT (K14-Cre), RE and KO compared; Liver-RE (Alfp-Cre) as second tissue. 8-week-old females. **Sampling:** timed, interfollicular epidermis RNA-seq at six time points 4 h apart over 24 h, n = 3-4 mice per time point and genotype, in 12:12 LD and again after 6-7 days of DD (156-176 h); ear-snip series every 4 h for 20 h in individual mice in DD. Single tissue per mouse (no paired tissues).

## Method (if any)
JTK_CYCLE per genotype; comparison of phase, amplitude and overlap of rhythmic gene sets; metabolic cages and IntelliCage for feeding and drinking.

## Key results
- LD: core clock oscillates in RE epidermis with preserved phase and slightly lower amplitude: "Notably, the phase of oscillation (i.e., time of the day when the expression of each gene reaches its zenith) was preserved" (Fig 1C-E). In Liver-RE the same clock genes oscillate "albeit with a 4-h phase advance" (Fig 1G, Koronowski 2019).
- Rhythmic genes: "approximately 13% of the homeostatic circadian transcriptome of WT epidermis (2,960 transcripts) was also circadian in the RE epidermis (392 transcripts)" (Fig 2A); shared genes keep ZT0-4 and ZT12-16 peaks with reduced amplitude. 224 genes rhythmic in WT, RE and KO (2-4 h phase shifts), "approximately 7% of the circadian transcriptome of the epidermis oscillates in a manner completely independent of BMAL1".
- Feeding is not the carrier: RE and KO feed and drink arrhythmically in LD, so the epidermal clock is light-driven; light entrains one tissue without BMAL1 anywhere else (Fig 3).
- DD: "only 53 of the 2,302 transcripts that were circadian in WT epidermis under D/D conditions still oscillated in the epidermis of RE mice" (Fig 4C). Individual-mouse ear snips show arrhythmia, not dispersion: "tissue arrhythmia, rather than phase dispersion between different mice, underlies the lack of BMAL1-driven oscillations" (Fig S4B).
- Epidermal-only Bmal1 partly rescues homeostasis (premature ageing) in otherwise arrhythmic mice.

## Not addressed / limits
- Phase is compared with WT epidermis at the same ZT, never with another tissue in the same animal; no inter-tissue offset in hours except the Liver-RE 4 h advance from the companion paper.
- Gene-set counts differ between companion papers (2,960 here vs 2,606 in Koronowski for WT epidermis); numbers should be cited per paper.
- Mouse, one tissue per animal, light-driven; does not address hormones or feeding-shifted conditions.

## Link to us
- **Intro claim(s):** tissue-specific rescue as experimental precedent for uncoupling (supported). Light, not feeding or other clocks, can synchronise one tissue; a tissue clock without extra-tissue Bmal1 persists in LD but is lost in DD.
- **Relation to clocksync:** background. Predicts that in a donor under rhythmic LD, a tissue can be phase-locked to the day yet carry lower coupling to the other compartments' shared clock state.
