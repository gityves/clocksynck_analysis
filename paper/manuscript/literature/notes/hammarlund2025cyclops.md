## Question and design
CYCLOPS 2.0, a covariate-aware version of CYCLOPS, applied to breast cancer and GTEx. Cohort-level, within-tissue ordering of untimed samples; no time coverage statement in the record.

## Method (if any)

- Breast tumours, especially luminal A: persistent but reprogrammed rhythms; CYCLOPS magnitude (CMag) tracks five-year survival; circadian eQTLs in adipose, muscle and skin (GTEx).
