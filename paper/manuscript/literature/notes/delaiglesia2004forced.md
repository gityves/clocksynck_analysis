# delaiglesia2004forced
**Ref:** de la Iglesia, Cambras, Schwartz, Diez-Noguera, Curr Biol 2004. DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2004.04.034. Type: biology. Lab: Schwartz / Diez-Noguera.
**Source:** full text

## Question and design
Can two SCN oscillators be dissociated in vivo? Male Wistar rats (2 months) in a symmetric 22 h LD cycle (11 h light 300 lux, 11 h dim red),
which produces two locomotor rhythms: one entrained at 22 h, one free-running with tau > 24 h (mean 24.97 +/- 0.03 h). Groups killed at four
defined phases (A: day for both rhythms, n = 6; B: night for both, n = 5; C: day for T22 but night for tau>24, n = 6; D: the reverse, n = 6),
SCN sections hybridised for Per1 (Per2 qualitatively the same) and Bmal1; dorsomedial/ventrolateral optical-density ratio (DM/VL). Two more groups released into
constant darkness (DD) and killed at extrapolated phases C (n = 7) and D (n = 5).
**Sampling:** timed, one animal one time; four phases chosen from each animal's behavioural rhythms.

## Key results
- Behaviour: "rats exposed to an artificially short 22 hr LD cycle express two stable circadian motor activity rhythms with different period lengths in individual animals."
- Phases A and B: Per1 high and Bmal1 low across the whole SCN at day, reverse at night (no regional split).
- Phase C: Per1 restricted to ventrolateral SCN, Bmal1 to dorsomedial; DM/VL ratios 0.59 +/- 0.04 (Per1) and 1.35 +/- 0.07 (Bmal1), 99% CI below and above 1.00. Phase D reversed: 1.55 +/- 0.09 (Per1), 0.87 +/- 0.03 (Bmal1).
- "Thus, under forced desynchronization, two regions within the SCN oscillate out of phase" (Results): ventrolateral follows the 22 h light cycle, dorsomedial the free-running rhythm.
- Persists in DD: DM/VL for Per1 0.76 +/- 0.06 at phase C, 1.40 +/- 0.09 at D (Bmal1 1.34 +/- 0.09 at C, 1.05 +/- 0.03 at D); "Thus, at least initially, the dorsomedial and ventrolateral subdivisions continue their out-of-phase oscillations even in the absence of the LD cycle."
- "Even though intercellular coupling between SCN subdivisions is lost, coupling within each subdivision is retained" (Discussion).

## Not addressed / limits
- Out-of-phase relation is shown at four fixed phases (about half a cycle apart), not as a continuous phase difference in hours; no individual time series of gene expression.
- Both subdivisions are inferred from antiphase Per1/Bmal1 patterns in autoradiographs; SCN only, rat, males; the paper states that re-fusion in DD is rapid, so desynchrony is transient.
- Human relevance (forced desynchrony of sleep and temperature) is a conjecture in the Discussion.

## Link to us
- **Intro claim(s):** supported. Cited for desynchrony within the SCN, as an example that coupling between subregions can be lost while coupling inside each is kept, driven by an exotic LD cycle.
- **Relation to clocksync:** background (internal desynchrony within a tissue); strengthens the point that one clock per compartment is an assumption: compartments inside a tissue can sit at different phases (cell types here).
