# fu2002circadian
**Ref:** Fu L, Pelicano H, Liu J, Huang P, Lee CC, Cell 2002;111:41-50. DOI 10.1016/S0092-8674(02)00961-3. Type: biology (mouse). Lab: Lee (Baylor).
**Source:** full text

## Question and design
mPer2m/m (PAS-B deletion) mice versus wild type; spontaneous neoplasia (n = 20 per genotype at 18 months), whole-body 4 Gy gamma irradiation (given at ZT10; irradiated n = 14 mutant, 20 WT), thymocyte apoptosis, liver Northern blots, MEF reporter assays. Host (germline) clock perturbed; tumours (lymphoma, teratoma, hyperplasia) arise in the mutant and are not assayed for clock genes, no tumour-versus-host comparison. **Sampling:** timed: liver at ZT2, 6, 10, 14, 18, 22 (three independent experiments) under LD; irradiation time course ZT10 to ZT1 next day; irradiation at ZT2, 10 or 18 for thymus.

## Method (if any)
Northern blot densitometry normalised to Gapdh, flow-cytometric apoptosis, luciferase promoter assays. No circadian inference.

## Key results
- Spontaneous: "By the age of 12 months, all mPer2m/m mice showed salivary gland hyperplasia" and all males had teratomas; 15 % of mutants died of lymphoma; none in WT at 18 months (p < 0.0001).
- After 4 Gy: "At 16 months after irradiation, 71% of irradiated mPer2m/m mice developed malignant lymphoma" versus 5 % of WT (10/14 vs 1/20; Table 1).
- Mutants show hair graying (all at 22 weeks), about 2-fold less thymocyte apoptosis at ZT10 and 18 h, p53 induction at 60 % and 40 % of WT at 4 and 6 h, less cytochrome c release.
- Clock gene induction: "However, mutation in mPer2 abolished the induction of all clock genes studied" after irradiation in liver (Fig 5).
- Rhythms: c-myc mRNA peaks at ZT14 and troughs at ZT10 in WT liver, "was consistently elevated at all ZT times" in the mutant; Cyclin D1 rhythm lost; Gadd45a falls to 20 % of WT at ZT10.
- Phase shift in the host clock: "but with a phase shift and a decrease in amplitude compared to the pattern in" WT, Bmal1 mRNA trough ZT6 and peak ZT18 in mutant (WT peak ZT2, trough ZT10; peak 70 % of WT).
- NPAS2/BMAL1 suppress c-myc P1/P2 promoters directly through E boxes ("The NPAS2/BMAL1 Heterodimers Suppress c-myc Promoters Directly").
- Context quoted from earlier work: "the proliferation of tumor cells follows autonomous circadian patterns that are out of phase with nontumor cells" (Klevecz 1987, Barbason 1995, cited, not tested).

## Not addressed / limits
- Whole-body mutants: the contribution of tumour-intrinsic versus host (e.g. systemic arrhythmia, behaviour) clock loss is not separated; no tumour clock measurement.
- Single irradiation time (ZT10) for cancer endpoints; six irradiated mutants were lost in a flood, so n = 14.
- Gene-expression rhythms rest on three Northern replicates per genotype, no formal rhythm test; mice are mutants of one allele, and Per2 also acts outside the clock.
- No human data.
