# paschos2012obesity
**Ref:** Paschos et al., Nature Medicine 2012 (18:1768-1777). DOI 10.1038/nm.2979. Type: biology (cell-type-specific knockout, energy balance). Lab: FitzGerald (UPenn).
**Source:** full text

## Question and design
Does the adipocyte clock shape energy balance? Bmal1 (Arntl) conditional allele x aP2-Cre (main line, Ad-Arntl-/-; male C57BL/6J, littermate Cre-negative controls) and x adiponectin-Cre (second line); myeloid control line (LysM-Cre, M-Arntl-/-) because aP2-Cre also hits macrophages; hepatocyte and islet knockouts are cited as comparators, not run. Clock genes in epididymal WAT and interscapular/subscapular BAT, hypothalamus, plasma; behaviour, calorimetry, feeding. **Sampling:** timed series in constant darkness, ad libitum feeding, 20-week-old mice, n = 4 per time point and group for the clock-gene panels (Fig 1i,j); number of time points not stated in the text read.

## Method (if any)
None. qPCR, permutation test for dampening of clock-gene rhythms (Supplementary Methods).

## Key results
- **Driver and targeting.** "In the presence of Cre recombinase, 75% and 50% of Arntl was excised in primary adipocytes isolated from white epididymal fat and brown interscapular fat, respectively" (PCR); Arntl mRNA 82% and 74% lower in white and brown adipocytes, protein not detectable. Recombination elsewhere: "We tested other tissues from these mice for loss of Arntl and found excision in peritoneal macrophages but not any other tissues examined" (liver, muscle, hypothalamus, adrenal negative by PCR, Fig 1h). Excision is lower in whole WAT "as a result of existence of cells other than adipocytes in WAT".
- **Targeted cells' clock, via whole tissue.** Controls oscillate in WAT and BAT; in the knockout the rhythm of Nr1d1 and Per3 is dampened in WAT (P < 0.01) and of Per2, Per3 and Dbp in BAT (P < 0.05); Npas2 and Cry1 up. The authors conclude the oscillator is "severely compromised" in both adipocyte types. Adipocytes are not read separately from the tissue.
- **Remaining rhythm in whole tissue, non-targeted cells.** "The lack of complete loss of oscillation of clock genes in WAT may reflect the function of remnant Arntl expression from the incomplete excision of the gene by Cre recombinase, as well as the existence of cells other than adipocytes in the tissue." Per1 and Per2 stay rhythmic with unaltered amplitude in WAT, read by the authors as Per1 and Per2 "do not depend on the intrinsic circadian clock of the white adipocyte to oscillate", with systemic signals as the proposed driver (as in the liver knockouts). No phase or amplitude estimate for non-adipocyte cells is made.
- **Other tissues and behaviour.** Locomotor rhythm normal (the SCN of regular-diet knockouts drove normal activity rhythms in LD and DD, Supplementary Fig 4). Hypothalamic clock and clock-output genes "showed low amplitude oscillations in the hypothalamus, with no differences between" genotypes (Supplementary Fig 6d). Behaviour that changes: food intake shifts to the light phase, about 60% of daily intake at night versus about 70% in controls, same total calories; hypothalamic Npy and Agrp up at CT4; obesity on regular and high-fat diet. In knockout mice on a high-fat diet, restricting food to the light period gives significantly more weight gain than restricting to the dark period at equal calories (P < 0.05, Fig 4a).
- Other peripheral clocks (liver, muscle, adrenal) are not measured in the text read, only tested for Cre excision.

## Not addressed / limits
- aP2-Cre also recombines in macrophages; macrophage knockout does not reproduce obesity, but whole-WAT clock genes in the myeloid line are not reported in the text read.
- Feeding time is shifted in the knockout, so residual WAT rhythms (Per1, Per2) can be driven by feeding (inference of ours; the paper attributes them to systemic signals) rather than by non-adipocyte autonomous clocks; no restricted-feeding or ex vivo explant clock test.
- Whole-tissue qPCR with n = 4 per time point and an unstated number of time points: coarse for phase.
- Partial recombination (50-75%) makes the targeted cells lost their clock statement itself approximate.

## Link to us
- **Intro claim(s):** cell-type-specific deletion perturbs organismal timing through feedback (adipocyte to hypothalamus) without breaking the SCN clock: supports inter-organ coupling. For within-tissue organisation: **weak, partly informative about autonomy**: a partial residual whole-WAT rhythm after adipocyte Bmal1 loss (Per1, Per2 intact) points to non-adipocyte cells or systemic drive keeping a clock, but cannot separate coupling from autonomy.
- **Relation to clocksync:** background. Human adipose single-cell data (our stromal-vascular result: stromal cells share most host state) is the natural counterpart; adipocytes themselves are poorly captured in snRNA and are the targeted cell here.
