# tahara2012vivo
**Ref:** Tahara et al., Curr Biol 2012 (22:1029-1034). DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2012.04.009. Type: biology / method (in vivo imaging). Lab: Shibata.
**Source:** full text

## Question and design
In vivo PER2::LUC imaging of kidney, liver and submandibular gland in the same mice (6 time points over a day,
luciferin s.c.); sham (n = 7) vs thermal SCN lesion (n = 10), 1 month in DD; then restricted feeding (12 days) and
2 weeks of free feeding (n = 8). **Sampling:** 4 h intervals over one day per session; repeated sessions in the same mice.

## Method (if any)
Cosinor fit per organ per mouse: amplitude, peak, goodness-of-fit index (< 0.1 = rhythmic); free-running period from
the peak drift between sessions.

## Key results
- Explants reset clocks: placing the tissue in culture "resets the phase and synchronizes the population of circadian oscillators in peripheral tissues" (Intro, Fig S1); all six SCNx livers were rhythmic ex vivo, including four arrhythmic in vivo.
- SCN lesion: "Circadian oscillations were detected in the kidney, liver, and submandibular gland studied in about half of the SCN-lesioned, behaviorally arrhythmic mice. However, their amplitude was decreased in these organs." (Summary)
- Organ order without the SCN: rhythmic organs peak at similar clock times in sham and SCNx (kidney 15.3 vs 14.8 h; liver 15.5 vs 16.4 h; gland 15.8 vs 15.6 h), larger spread across SCNx mice.
- Feeding as substitute drive: restricted feeding synchronised SCNx peripheral clocks; after 2 weeks of free feeding, rhythmicity kept but amplitude decreased, organs still at a similar phase within a mouse, broader spread between mice. Free-running periods 23.71 (kidney), 23.68 (liver), 23.70 h (gland): organs drift together.

## Not addressed / limits
- One-day sessions at 4 h resolution; three organs; thermal lesions vary; liver signal partly overlaps other tissue.

## Link to us
- **Intro claim(s):** without the central clock, organs keep oscillating with lower amplitude; a feeding rhythm re-imposes a common phase.
- **Relation to clocksync:** supports; caution for A319 (Izumo explant phases include a culture reset, stronger in SCN-less mice). Organs with near-equal periods drift together for weeks: a reason why a shared phase can persist without a drive (A319 H319.1 borderline E).

## Ideas
- Equal organ periods mean drift between organs is slow; desynchrony in DD (Husse, Izumo) may be mostly cell dephasing within tissues plus phase noise, not period differences.
