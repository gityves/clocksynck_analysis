# huang2026retraction
**Ref:** Huang Z, Zeng L, Ruan Z et al. (senior/corresponding: Mok, Scheiermann, Lévi, Yang, Zhang), "Time-of-day immunochemotherapy in non-small cell lung cancer: a randomized phase 3 trial" (LungTIME-C01), Nat Med 32:1233-1240 (2026), published 2 Feb 2026, DOI 10.1038/s41591-025-04181-w; RETRACTED 24 June 2026 by Retraction Note, Nat Med 32:2691 (2026), DOI 10.1038/s41591-026-04508-1. Type: clinical (retracted trial). Lab: Hunan Cancer Hospital (Zhang); Lévi and Scheiermann as chronobiology co-authors.
**Source:** retraction note full text (nature.com); original article: abstract only (paywalled; the methods, figures and the protocol were not read)

## Question and design
Single-centre, open-label randomized phase 3 (NCT05549037): 210 treatment-naive stage IIIC-IV NSCLC patients without driver mutations, 1:1 to early or late time-of-day (ToD), defined by giving the first four cycles of an anti-PD-1 agent before or after 15:00 h. Primary endpoint PFS; secondary OS and ORR. **Sampling:** n/a (clinical trial; time of infusion is the randomized factor).

## Key results (original claims, from the abstract)
- "After a median follow-up of 28.7 months, the median PFS was 11.3 months (95% confidence interval (CI) = 9.2-13.4) in the early ToD group and 5.7 months (95% CI = 5.2-6.2) in the late ToD group", HR 0.40 (95% CI 0.29-0.55; P < 0.001).
- Median OS 28.0 months (95% CI NE-NE) early vs 16.8 months (95% CI 13.7-19.9) late, HR 0.42 (95% CI 0.29-0.60; P < 0.001) (abstract).
- Mechanism claim: morning circulating CD8+ T cells rose in the early group and fell in the late group (P < 0.001); activated (CD38+HLA-DR+) to exhausted (TIM-3+PD-1+) CD8+ T-cell ratio higher in the early group (abstract).

## Retraction (24 June 2026)
- Reason, registration and protocol: "After publication, concerns were raised about substantial changes made to the study registration on (NCT05549037)." Endpoints, eligibility criteria, sample size and study design "appear to have been inconsistently reported and modified over time"; the translated protocol was dated 2022 but cited studies published in 2023 and 2024; further discrepancies between the Chinese original and translated protocols; the authors attributed these to "administrative errors rather than prospectively documented amendments".
- Reason, data patterns: PFS curve smoother than usual in similar large phase 3 trials; no censoring in the first year; no adverse event leading to treatment discontinuation; similar rates of immune-related adverse events in the two arms despite different efficacy; randomization on the day of treatment for almost all patients (source data); deviations from fixed-calendar imaging due to COVID-19 and cycle-based assessment (lack of adherence to RECIST timing).
- Verdict: "due to the amount and nature of the problems identified, the editors no longer have confidence in the integrity of the results." The note names design problems (registration and protocol) and data-pattern problems; it does not use the words "data integrity" beyond this sentence, and no misconduct finding is stated.
- Seventeen authors (including Lévi, Scheiermann, Mok, Zhang) agree with the retraction; eleven did not respond to the editors.
- Earlier editorial action: Editor's Note of 19 Feb 2026 on inconsistencies between the ClinicalTrials.gov record and the published protocol.

## Not addressed / limits
- Full original methods and the discrepancies in the registry history (reported by press and commentators, e.g. observational/interventional record changes in March 2024) were not read from a primary source here.
