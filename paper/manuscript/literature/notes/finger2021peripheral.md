# finger2021peripheral
**Ref:** Finger and Kramer, Genes & Development 2021 (35:304-306). DOI 10.1101/gad.348305.121. Type: review (Outlook commentary on Sinturel et al. 2021). Lab: Kramer (Charite, Berlin).
**Source:** full text

## Question and design
Three-page commentary: does the SCN synchronise peripheral clocks, and do peripheral clocks stay coherent without it? No data. It restates Sinturel et al. 2021 (RT-Biolumicorder, SCN-lesioned and hepatocyte clock-only mice, constant conditions) and places it against Koronowski 2019.

## Method (if any)
None.

## Key results
- Statement of the question: coherence between peripheral clocks needs the SCN, coherence within a peripheral tissue does not. Peripheral clocks "depend on the SCN only to establish synchrony among but not within peripheral body clocks". In SCN-lesioned animals in constant conditions whole-body bioluminescence amplitude is strongly reduced, so the SCN synchronises non-SCN body clocks.
- Interpretation that is the commentary's own, not a measurement: liver clock oscillates robustly with similar amplitude and period in SCN-proficient and SCN-deficient mice; rhythms persist with reduced amplitude in hepatocyte clock-only mice. "peripheral clocks are not mere servile oscillators driven by the SCN" and they maintain coherent rhythms "due to coupling among oscillator cells". Figure 1 labels peripheral oscillators "weakly coupled, yet autonomous, body clocks".
- Explains the discrepancy with Koronowski 2019: tissue samples from different animals per time point, so "low-amplitude peripheral rhythms have likely been missed due to dephasing between animals rather than between hepatocyte oscillators". Within-animal (real-time) recording is needed.
- Open questions: mediator of coupling among hepatocytes and other cells (answered in part by finger2021intercellular with TGF-beta), whether coupling exists in all peripheral tissues, whether signals from other body clocks are needed; cites Guan 2020 for intercellular communication between cell types in controlling rhythmic liver gene expression.

## Not addressed / limits
- Commentary: no new data; coupling is inferred, not measured, in Sinturel (hepatocyte coherence from a population reporter, not single cells). Note the logic: persistence of weak rhythm in hepatocyte-only mice shows autonomy plus some synchrony, not which cell population paces.
- Time scale: constant conditions over weeks, as in Sinturel.

## Link to us
- **Intro claim(s):** SCN synchronises organs (amplitude collapse of whole-body signal after lesion) while within-tissue coherence persists (supports organ arrangement lost, tissue clock kept in framing our A315 brain-death result; paraphrase, not a quote).
- **Relation to clocksync:** background. The between-animal dephasing argument supports reading each donor against its own other tissues rather than group profiles (rule already in sinturel card). The sentence on the SCN synchronising body clocks while tissue amplitude persists is the closest conceptual precedent for A315, but the time scale there is weeks in mice, versus hours to days of ventilation in humans.
