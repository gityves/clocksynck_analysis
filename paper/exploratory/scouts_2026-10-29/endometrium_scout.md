## Suitability

- **GSE51981**: best. Largest (64 proliferative vs 82 secretory sample labels; 20 vs 14 healthy), one sample per patient presumably [U], uniform platform (GPL570), phase label by histology/dating. Weakness: whole tissue (stromal mix; epithelium fraction varies with phase), endometriosis and pathology covariates, no hormones or cycle day, and bulk mixes epithelium and stroma so the "epithelial coherence" of GSE266265 is only indirectly replicated. Pre-specify the primary stratum (non-endometriosis, no pathology: prolif 20 vs secretory 14+2) with all-sample sensitivity.
- **GSE141549**: second. 24 prolif vs 43 secretory, two platforms (needs platform stratum), "medication" 53 samples excluded; healthy-only 7 vs 16 (<10 prolif). Usable only pooled (endometriosis + healthy) with disease as covariate.
- **GSE6364**: small (11 vs 26), same platform as GSE51981 (the two series may overlap in patients, not checked [U]). Replication only.
- **GSE4888**: proliferative 4. Unsuitable for a two-group test; ordering within secretory sub-phases only.
- **Ulrich**: not independent (15 of 20 donors are Garcia-Alonso and Wang). Phase is not machine-readable in Census. Useful as the pooled single-cell reference of Garcia-Alonso + Wang + 4 new donors (19 donors), not as a primary.
- **Wang, Garcia-Alonso**: 10 and 5 donors, and both are inside Ulrich/HECA. Single cell secondary only.
- **HECA**: largest single-cell set with phases, but integrates earlier cohorts and must be checked for the GSE266265 donors (and Census absence means a CELLxGENE download of a large h5ad). Possible a second-stage replication if overlap is excluded.
- **Tan**: no phase; exclude.
- **No laser-captured epithelium-only samples** in any of the bulk series (all whole-tissue biopsies) [V for GSE51981, GSE141549, GSE4888 and GSE6364 titles/sources; no LCM terms].

## Recommendation

Primary confirmatory dataset: **GSE51981** (GEO, GPL570, 146 samples with proliferative vs secretory labels, >=10 donors per phase in every stratum except healthy early-secretory). Primary contrast across donors: proliferative (n=64) vs secretory (early+mid+late, n=82); sensitivity: non-endometriosis subset (prolif 35 incl. pathology) and mid-secretory only. Replication: GSE141549 pooled (24 vs 43, platform stratum). Because bulk mixes cell types, state in the registration that the test is on whole endometrium and compute a stromal/epithelial composition covariate (e.g. a luminal-epithelium vs stroma marker score) without touching the clock panel. Must resolve before registering: (1) GSE51981 files (CEL only in RAW.tar? check supplementary for processed matrix; the series matrix is in the SOFT file) and the age field absent; (2) probe-to-gene mapping for the 68-gene clock panel on GPL570; (3) donor overlap between GSE51981 and GSE6364 or GSE4888 (not checked).
