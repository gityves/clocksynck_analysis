## Discrepancies and issues found

1. **FM:51 is stale for A164.** FM:51 quotes T2D [-0.021, 0.060] and obesity [-0.081, 0.089]; CL:74 and RP:3249-3251 give the post-A202 [-0.037, +0.076] and [-0.083, +0.091]. VT:140-141 keeps the A164 originals, with VT:259 giving the re-read. Use the A202 values.
2. **Headline vs brain death.** CL:30 still says "not as body-wide internal desynchrony" and counts "loss of daily cues"; CL:67 and RP:260 show brain-dead donors lose the order (0.161 vs 0.678). NA:90-91 proposes the new wording but CL is unchanged (AU:10-11).
3. **A136 wording in NA:89** "glucocorticoids do not explain it (A136)" is stronger than RP:2741-2745: the GR gate failed (H136.1 p 0.098, Holm 0.197), H136.2 MDE 0.515, steroid-user sensitivity p 0.021, and only the PER1/PER2 route is excluded (H136.3). It is also the same 79+79 donors as A132b (AU:12).
4. **A190 in CL:60** is rated B and quoted for gate p 0.0018, but RP:2903 states A190 is "not evidence of a coupled adrenal clock" (corrected gate p_pairing 0.672). FM:34 still reads "strong / qualified". A181 (RP:2617-2624) says fetal inter-organ sharing cannot be tested at the Cao design; CL does not cite A181.
5. **CL:59 attributes "earlier windows show no clock-shaped state" to A121**; A121 H121.1 FAILED underpowered (RP:827-828) and the embryo panel was assigned to Extended Data (RP:835). FM:35 cites A108/A121 for this (p_acrophase 0.63/0.55/0.33 are archive gates, not RP).
6. **CL:53 / headline "ageing"**: the only registered equivalence test (A180) failed (RP:2552). A19 gamma is own-noise, not coupling or offset. A130 drift -0.339 h per decade, p 0.054 (RP:385), is not excluded.
7. **CL row 5 omits A153** (RP:1880: "cannot state 'coupling preserved in disease' as a tested claim"; every eligible MDE > 0.2). AU:13 raised this; CL:74-75 unchanged. "Chronic disease" = GTEx T2D and obesity (metabolic organs) plus 14 TOST rows.
8. **CL:92 (row 7.1) labels A124 as blood**; RP:1263 gives muscle GSE106800, 11 subjects (AU:9).
9. **CL:81 (A54 -1.43 h)** is the post hoc 15-series value; the A54 discovery is -1.50 h (AU:11). FM:59 repeats -1.43.
10. **Equivalence bound +-0.15 on d*** (A153 bound, RE:1933, 2291) is a convention, not a biological threshold; with c about 0.39 (A150, RP:2575) it is large. The A164 module MDE of 0.083 is the more informative number. A208 H208.1 MDE 0.032 shows what a tighter bound could reach on GTEx with pooled design.
11. **A208 (sex) is registered and passed** (RP:3410-3424) but absent from CL and FM; the ledger addendum (CL:123-134) covers only cancer.
12. **A164 specificity companion** is tissue-based (nerve, skin), not pseudo-clock genes; CLAUDE.md requires a specificity companion for every disease contrast. For an equivalence claim the companion supports "organ-specific" only (RE:2291), and T2D module minus companion p 0.644 is not an equivalence either.
13. A164 per-cohort: obese vent +0.067 and fast -0.048 (RP:1902); coverage of the A202 interval was verified at the A164 design only (RP:3253 "Thinning defines the truth on the estimator's scale, so this stays a stated limit").
14. A111 SCN-specific Bmal1 KO (mouse, 6 vs 6): liver-heart coherence 0.947 vs 0.912, no coupling loss (RP:408-410, "small sample and 2 time points"). It is the only direct central-drive perturbation and it does not reproduce body-wide desynchrony; relevant to the "only when central drive is lost" reading. Not in the ledger.

## Summary lists

**Equivalence-supported (registered equivalence bound, passed, interval validated):**
- T2D, inter-organ coupling of metabolic organs in GTEx (A164 H164.1, A202): +0.019 [-0.037, +0.076], bound +-0.15.
- Obesity, same (A164 H164.2, A202): +0.004 [-0.083, +0.091].
- Sex, coupling and offsets in GTEx (A208, secondary): d* +0.020 [-0.003, +0.043]; offset +0.043 h [-0.104, +0.189]. Not in ledger.
- 14 named single-cell compartment x disease rows (A180, secondary, post-estimate): phase 7 of 8 within +-1.5 h, amplitude 7 of 10 within 1.5-fold. Temperature as a cause of the cue-loss drop (A110 H110.3, bound 0.05).

**Rest on non-detection only (MDE stated, no equivalence, or equivalence failed):**
- Ageing, peripheral (A19 MDE 0.20 per decade; A180 equivalence FAILED).
- Disease atlases in general (A153 no primary; A165 0 of 89 with median phase MDE 2.20 h; A167 0 of 3, MDE 0.29-0.43).
- Loss of wall time in Hardy 4 (A109c, 80 % MDE 0.208).
- Order of phase relations kept in ventilated/Hardy 4 donors (positive rho, secondary and post hoc; registered A109 H109.2 failed).
- Malignant-cell coupling to host (A138 H138.1 FAIL, MDE about 0.2).
- Embryo (A108/A121; gate borderline, onset not shown, n 16), mouse.
- Feeding as a cause (A110 H110.5, 2 pairs, MDE 0.20).

**Untested:**
- Night shift (A124 MDE 7.3 h) and forced desynchrony (A156 MDE 13.1 h): uninformative.
- Human jet lag or any multi-organ shift with different baseline phases (A201 mouse, H201.1 not estimable by 0.03 h).
- Season; other chronic diseases in GTEx (anything beyond T2D, obesity); human fetal inter-organ coupling (A181: design cannot test).
- Also untested as equivalence: coupling or offsets with age.

**Counter-cases (already in the docs):** brain death (A132b, A136; breaks the order body-wide, mechanism open), brain and digestive tract apart (A142, A142 H142.2), psoriasis (A183; uninvolved skin also late, A115), tumour coherence loss (A127, A166, A171, A173).

**Positive (not absence) cancer results used for "keeps shared time in cancer":** A138 H138.4 (0.365, powered, clock-specific), A227-A229 (immune and endothelial compartments, margins of 0.03-0.04 over pseudo q95).

## Bottom line for the headline

Only metabolic disease (T2D, obesity; plus sex) has a genuine registered equivalence bound that passed. "Ageing", "chronic disease" in general, "development" and "loss of daily cues" are partly non-detections, partly positive readouts, and partly one-organ or mouse results. The "loss of daily cues" clause is a positive coherence result against nulls with a registered primary failure (H109.2) and a post hoc brain-death split. Human multi-organ misalignment (shift work, jet lag) is untested.
