## Summary

- **Usable without remapping: none.** No cohort has deposited spliced / unspliced, exon / intron or velocyto loom matrices on GEO, the atlas site,
  Zenodo-type repositories (no hit), ArrayExpress/ENA (Kildisiute's ENA records are tumours) or cellxgene (only the Han HCL collection
  exists; no layers expected). Verified by listing for GEO (5 series, 6 sample listings), by opening the local h5ad (Kildisiute: no layers).
- **Cohorts where velocity matrices exist in the authors' hands:** kameneva (velocyto 0.17.13 looms, referenced in the GitHub notebook; location
  unverified), sasaki (velocyto 0.17 matrices, not deposited), kildisiute (velocyto, not deposited). These three are the candidates for a
  request to the authors (draft only; the user decides). Fetuses behind them: kameneva 11 (looms evidenced for 6 samples), sasaki 7, kildisiute 5.
- **Download size without remapping:** 0 GB for the matrices, since nothing usable is deposited.
- **Remapping (not to be done; for the decision):** open fastq in ENA (all 10x 3'; velocyto/STARsolo-Velocyto or kallisto|bustools lamanno
  from fastq, human reference):
  - kameneva PRJNA621175: about 80-85 GB adrenal (112 GB whole project), 11 fetuses;
  - wang PRJNA705264: about 200 GB adrenal (367 GB whole), 10 fetuses;
  - dong PRJNA573097: about 124 GB adrenal (670 GB whole), 4 fetuses;
  - sasaki PRJNA797105: about 120 GB for the adrenal / testis-adrenal runs (218 GB whole), GSE193672 fetuses only; the GSE291218 human fetal reads
    are stated as not submitted;
  - kildisiute: not possible (controlled EGA); han: unverified (SRA exists, size unknown, Microwell-seq).
  Total for the four open cohorts: about 530 GB of fastq (adrenal samples only, estimate from ENA fastq_bytes; sample-to-fetus mapping for sasaki
  unverified), covering about 32 fetuses. Compute: one alignment per library, order of 1-2 h on 16 threads each, with a human STAR/cellranger
  index (about 30 GB RAM); exceeds the 500 GB data cap unless streamed and deleted per sample.
- Not found for any cohort: deposited BAMs (ENA lists fastq; only the Kildisiute tumour records carry BAMs).
