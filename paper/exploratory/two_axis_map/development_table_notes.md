# Developmental loudness table: provenance and checks against PAPER_PLAN 2.4

Table: `development_table.csv` (74 rows). Only numbers already in output CSVs; no resampling. Built by a scratch script that reads the CSVs named below (not kept in the repo). Paths are under `paper/exploratory/`.

## Which CSV column gave which number

- **A242 (whole adrenal, bulk, 4 rows):** `A242_fetal_adrenal_replication/output/l1.csv` (C, p, matched_q95, responsive_q95, clock_specific); `feasibility_passrates.csv` (`pass_rate` at settings a1 / a0.75 / a0.5); `position_in_adult.csv` (`pct_adult_at_or_below` -> column `adult_pct_at_or_below_1_0.75_0.5`; `size_rel_adult` is derived = `fetal_C / adult_median_C` at a1, a ratio of coherences, not an amplitude fraction); n from `design.csv`.
- **A244 (adrenal cortex, medulla; all / <12 wpc / >=12 wpc, 6 rows):** `A244_fetal_adrenal_classes/output/{l1,feasibility_passrates,position,design}.csv`; `size_rel_adult` derived = `C / adult_median` (`position.csv`, only cortex all and cortex late exist there, so only those two have it; positive values only). Cortex "all" row carries the core-loop columns from A266 `l1.csv` (`C`, `p`) and `verdicts.csv` (`verdict`).
- **A264 / A266 rows (adrenal cortex):** `A264_fetal_adrenal_covariates/output/{l1,verdicts,feasibility_passrates}.csv` (`cortex:-PER1-NR1D1`, `cortex:steroid`, `cortex:age`); `A266_fetal_adrenal_drive/output/{l1,verdicts,feasibility_passrates}.csv` (`cortex:core`, 11 genes). Power at 1/0.75/0.5 from each file's `pass_rate`. Layer assigned only to the two core-loop variants.
- **A243 (heart, kidney, lung, liver; all / early / late, 12 rows):** `A243_fetal_organs_pooled/output/{l1,verdicts,feasibility_passrates,position_in_adult,design}.csv`, same columns as A242. `early` = <12 wpc, `late` = >=12 wpc (from the entry text; the CSV only has the labels). Lung all, lung early and liver all carry the core-loop columns from A269 (`l1.csv` C and p; `verdicts.csv` verdict; lung early has no verdict row, text copied from `l1.csv` clock_specific).
- **A269 rows (lung core, liver core, lung early core, lung gc, liver gc):** `A269_fetal_organs_drive/output/{l1,verdicts,feasibility_passrates}.csv`.
- **A307 rows (6):** `A307_fetal_gc_pattern/output/tests.csv` (`value`, `p`, `matched_q95`, `clock_specific`, `p_holm` in note) and `power.csv` (n, MDE). No responsive q95 and no adult power table exist for this entry: pass-rate columns blank.
- **A237 (Cardoso-Moreira bulk, 4-20 wpc, 4-8 wpc, 13-20 wpc, 5 postnatal controls, L2):** statistic, p, q95s, clock_specific from `A237_fetal_organs/output/l1.csv`; verdict from `verdicts.csv`; prenatal power at 1/0.75/0.5 from `A240_fetal_amplitude/output/curve.csv` (arm `cm`); `bound_rel_adult` = `A240 bound.csv` column `a80` (smallest scaled-clock fraction detected in >=0.8 of subsamples) for failing prenatal readouts with pass rate at 1 >= 0.8. Postnatal controls: pass rate at 1 only, from `scouts_2026-11-10/feasibility_fetal/output/passrates_cm_day_sex.csv` (`pass_rate`, adjust = adjusted); no scaled power exists for them. The L2 row (organs of one fetus) uses `verdicts.csv` stat (pairing z) and `p`; q95 columns blank (percentiles are in the note).
- **A238 (gonad 4 classes):** `A238_fetal_gonad/output/{l1,verdicts,design}.csv`; power and a80 from A240 `curve.csv` / `bound.csv` arm `gonad` (breast-atlas stand-ins, labels `breast:<class>:all`). Endothelium: layer left blank (Holm 0.056, "suggestive, not established").
- **A239 (Kang brain mean):** `A239_fetal_brain/output/{l1,verdicts,design}.csv`. Prenatal: power and a80 from A240 (arm `kang`). pcw13-22, infancy-childhood, from 13 y (labelled adolescent-adult), postnatal control: pass rate at 1 only from `scouts_2026-11-10/feasibility_fetal/output/passrates_kang_all.csv`. These windows are descriptive in the entry; verdict "descriptive".
- **A309 (mouse E15-E18.75, 5 organs + 2 GC-pattern rows):** `A309_mouse_embryo_organs/output/l1.csv` (C, p, q95s, `adult_a1 / a075 / a05`, `clock_specific`, verdict) and `gc_pattern.csv` (L, W).
- **A236 (2 rows):** `A236_embryo_running_clock/output/h236_primary.csv` (S1, critical value) and `h236_3_companions.csv` (R, q95s). Timed statistics, not the untimed C. A236 p for R is the clock-time permutation p_clock (0.0138); H236.3's p against responsive sets is 0.189.

Rules applied:
- `clock_specific`: the CSV flag mapped to yes/no only when p < 0.05; "na" when p >= 0.05.
- `bound_rel_adult`: filled only for failing estimates whose pass rate at 1 is >= 0.8: A240 `a80` where A240 covers the readout, else the smallest of {0.5, 0.75, 1} with pass rate >= 0.8 (derived from the three tabulated settings). Blank otherwise (including PASS rows and underpowered fails).
- `layer`: signal = covariation present but core loop (without PER1/PER2/NR1D1) not clock-specific; clock = clock-specific on the core loop; none = failing readout with p >= 0.05 and pass rate at 1 >= 0.8; "clock (postnatal)" for postnatal controls and postnatal brain windows. Blank where the rule does not decide (core loop not run, suggestive p < 0.05 failures, A242 whole-adrenal rows, mouse liver).
- Adrenal 0.5 / 0.75 bounds, lung core 0.5: read from `feasibility_passrates.csv` (cortex:core 0.63 / 0.995; lung:core 0.84 / 1.0).
- Fields filled from prose, not CSV: `n_individuals = 16` for A236 (rows 73-74 of the table, and the A309 `gc_pattern` rows copy 16 from `l1.csv`); `early` / `late` = <12 / >=12 wpc labels (CSVs carry only the names); stage "6-21 wpf" (gonad) and "E15-E18.75".

## Claims in PAPER_PLAN 2.4 that the CSVs do not support or that differ

1. **Adrenal numbers are attributed to A243.** The table row says "adrenal cortex, lung, liver ... half to three quarters of the adult size (A243)". A243 has no adrenal; the adrenal band comes from A242 (whole adrenal: adult-subsample percentiles 3.0 / 16.5 / 83.5 % at 1 / 0.75 / 0.5 for >=12 wpc, 1.0 / 42.0 / 100 % for all). A244 `position.csv` has only the full-clock percentile for the cortex (1.0 %), so a cortex-specific "half to three quarters" is not in any CSV.
2. **Cortex signal is early only.** A244 cortex >=12 wpc C 0.169, p 0.266 (adult pass 0.91), <12 wpc C 0.652. A242 whole adrenal >=12 wpc passes (C 0.552, p 0.022). The plan's stage row does not carry this window split.
3. **Lung <12 wpc core loop is positive.** A269 `lung:early:core` C 0.647, p 0.014, above both companions (clock_specific True, 13 fetuses, adult pass 0.975 / 0.845 / 0.505; descriptive, not registered). By the layer rule this is "clock", which conflicts with "core loop excluded down to 0.5 in lung". The plan's lung statement holds only for lung pooled (core C 0.473, p 0.060, pass 0.84 at 0.5).
4. **Liver core loop is not excluded.** `liver:core` C 0.686, p 0.0086 (Holm 0.017), above matched (99.5th) but below responsive q95 0.712. The signal on the core loop is larger than on the 14 genes (0.583). "Carried by PER1, PER2, NR1D1" is therefore not supported for liver; the plan lists liver with the other two but gives core bounds only for adrenal and lung (correct as far as it goes). A307 liver: L p 0.117, W p 0.042 below matched q95: not resolved (consistent with the plan).
5. **"None: a clock of 0.75 to 1 times the adult one excluded (A240)"** summarises A240 a80 (0.75 in 9 readouts, 1.0 in 9). Heart pooled (A243) has pass rate 0.80 at 0.5 (bound 0.5); kidney pooled 0.995 at 0.75 but 0.25 at 0.5. Brain forebrain and hindbrain have a80 1.0 and 0.75 (A240 bound.csv). The plan's single range hides this spread.
6. **"Medulla, heart, kidney, brain: none shown"** omits readouts the entries call suggestive and that are in the CSV: gonad endothelium (C 0.523, p 0.014, Holm 0.056, clock-specific), liver 4-20 wpc (p 0.042), hindbrain 4-8 wpc (p 0.040), lung >=12 wpc (p 0.040) and liver >=12 wpc (p 0.0245). All fail Holm; rows are present, layer blank.
7. **Mouse row.** (a) Hepatocytes are clock-specific on the 14-gene set (C 0.642, p 0.0023, 99.0 / 99.5 percentile); the core loop without PER1/PER2/NR1D1 was not run, so "clock layer" is not confirmed by the plan's own rule. (b) A236 is cited as an entry, but its CSVs are negative (S1 0.069, p 0.975; R 0.516, not clock-specific); it is not support. (c) Cardiomyocytes: pass 0.97 at 0.75 (consistent with "three quarters excluded"). (d) A309 liver L (GC link) is not clock-specific (0.347, 73.5th percentile), W passes. (e) Prose in `results_preliminary.md` (A309) says adrenocortical C is "above both companions' q95"; `l1.csv` has C 0.4066 below matched q95 0.4173 (above responsive q95 0.3766).
8. **Postnatal row: matches.** brain 0.735 (infancy-childhood) and 0.6625 (from 13 y; the plan's 0.663), A237 controls 0.539 to 0.841. Note: the A239 windows are descriptive, not registered, and A239's own control L2 failed (not clock-specific).
9. **Row 2 "brain 13-22 pcw C 0.047":** matches (0.04713, p 0.412, pass rate at 1 0.97).
10. **Plan sentences about A307:** matches (adrenal cortex W 0.083 PASS, lung W 0.076 PASS; lung L 0.204 PASS, cortex L not clock-specific). A307 has no adult power table, only an MDE (L 0.096-0.122, W 0.051-0.058).
11. **"Signal layer present" in lung and liver for the 14-gene set** is supported (A243 C 0.530 and 0.583, both PASS), and it survives adjustment for glucocorticoid-target score (A269 lung:gc 0.572, liver:gc 0.589). Not blind: A264, A266, A269, A307 are marked NOT BLIND in the entries.
