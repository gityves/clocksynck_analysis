# Scout: public gastric-cancer scRNA-seq for per-patient malignant / CAF / endothelial pseudobulks (2026-10-31)
Metadata only, blind: no expression value, clock gene or clock statistic was read. Only GEO family soft files, supplementary `filelist.txt`, one cell-metadata table (GSE206785, label columns only) and one sample xlsx were downloaded (about 0.6 MB, `$SCCC_DATA/scout_sc_gastric/`, PROVENANCE.md with sha256). Scripts in `scripts/` (run with `python -I`). Tables: `datasets.csv` (all datasets), `samples_all.csv` (per-GSM metadata, 842 rows), `GSE206785_patient_x_type.csv`, `GSE268238_sample_metadata.csv` (raw xlsx dump).

## Verdict
No public gastric set deposits an author malignant-cell label or cell-type table together with raw counts, except GSE206785, which has cell types but too few tumour epithelial cells and no malignant call. Candidates for >= 25 patients exist only if we annotate ourselves (clustering + CNV inference, as done for the archive scbc/scbc_full cohorts):

| 1 | GSE270680 (27-donor sc + spatial atlas) | 25 (26 tumour samples; +22 adjacent normal) | yes, 10x mtx per sample | no | no | tumour matrices 1.17 GB; with adjacent normal 2.03 GB (RAW.tar 3.3 GB incl. LN and blood) | stage I 4 / II 7 / III 15; Lauren intestinal 10 / indeterminate 9 / diffuse 7 |

| 3 | GSE150290 (Kim 2022) | 23 (design text says 24) | yes, 10x raw_gene_bc_matrices per sample (plus small QC txt) | no | no | 1.3 GB | Lauren intestinal 8 / diffuse 15; EGC 14 / AGC 8 / "ECG" 1 |

Estimate for ">= 25 patients with >= 50 malignant cells": cannot be confirmed without matrices. Only GSE270680 (25 tumour donors) is within one patient of 25, GSE183904 has 26; both would need malignant cells to be recovered by our CNV step, so a realistic yield is below the patient count (by analogy Wu 2021 breast: 9-11 of 26 patients with >= 50 author-labelled cancer cells). GSE150290 has about 2 k cells per sample so at most a minority will reach 50 malignant cells. Total counts for a pooled GSE270680 + GSE183904 + GSE150290 attempt: about 1.2 + 0.33 + 1.3 = 2.8 GB (tumour-only for GSE270680; add 0.86 GB for adjacent normal epithelium). Pooling is plausible only if the three cohorts are independent patients (separate Chinese / Korean / Singapore sites, not verified).

## Dataset notes
- **GSE183904 (Kumar 2022, PMID 34642171).** 40 GSM: tumour samples 29 (primary 26 patients, peritoneal tumour sample 3), normal 11. Overall design: 31 gastric malignancies; raw files withheld for privacy. Only per-sample csv.gz in the 329 MB RAW.tar. Cells per patient unknown until the csv headers are read (not done: the files hold expression).
- **GSE270680.** 77 GSM: tumour 26, adjacent normal 22, lymph node 15, blood 14; 10x 5' NovaSeq. Sample titles carry donor, stage and Lauren subtype. Raw reads not submitted (Chinese MOST rule). No cell annotation in GEO; possibly on the authors' repository (not checked). Adjacent normal gives normal epithelium and stroma for 22 donors.
- **GSE150290 (Kim 2022).** 52 GSM (23 tumour, 29 normal incl. unmatched normals Pat21, 25-29). Mostly early GC (14 EGC), which suits proliferation but limits tumour size. Cell calls absent.
- **GSE206785.** 111141 cells, 48 samples (24 patients, tumour + adjacent normal). `Type` labels (Epithelial, Fibroblast, Endothelial, Mural, ...) and `Annotation` (EP01-EP16 epithelial lineage states) are deposited, but there is no malignant flag. Tumour cells per class across 24 patients: Epithelial 2135, Fibroblast 3089, Endothelial 1423. Patients with >= 50 cells: Epithelial 13, Fibroblast 10, Endothelial 6, all three 3 (normal: 12 / 9 / 5 / 2). Insufficient. Raw reads in GSA HRA002336 (controlled). The deposited `scgex.txt.gz` (236 MB) normalisation was not checked.
- **Small sets (reject on n):** GSE163558 (3 primary + 1 adjacent normal + 6 metastases, 6 patients), GSE167297 (5 diffuse-type patients, superficial/deep layers), GSE134520 (9 patients, 1 EGC), GSE246662 (3 primary GC), GSE249874 (6 GC, 1.3 GB mtx), GSE158631 (3 patients, Smart-seq), GSE112302, GSE140182 (ascites), GSE281274 (CDH1 carriers, normal mucosa), GSE234209 (blood/ascites immune).
- **Sathe 2020, HRA000704 / OMIX:** not on GEO; Sathe has about 10 patients (paper), insufficient; HRA000704 is GSA-Human (controlled). Neither reachable under the download rules.

## Caveats and what could not be verified
- No deposited malignant-cell label in any candidate: the design requires our own CNV-based calling (as in the archive A86 pipeline); effective n per patient with >= 50 malignant cells is unknown until then. Same risk as the breast scout (Wu: 9-11 of 26).
- CAF and endothelial counts per patient: not tabulable without matrices or labels (only GSE206785, where they are low: fibroblast >= 50 in 10, endothelial in 6 of 24 tumours). The host reference (stromal-vascular) is therefore the likely limiting factor; GSE270680 5' libraries of whole tumour pieces should have more stroma, unverified.
- Whether per-sample files are raw UMI or normalised: not read (would require opening count files). Only GSE150290 explicitly names `raw_gene_bc_matrices`, GSE270680 and GSE163558 are 10x mtx (raw counts probable, unverified).
- Patient independence across GSE183904 / GSE270680 / GSE150290 / Kyushu series not checked; Kyushu series re-use samples (GSE254188 lists samples re-analysed from GSE201347 and GSE231539).
- Per-patient cell counts and Lauren/MSI/EBV/stage for GSE183904 and GSE150290 beyond the table above need the papers' supplementary tables (not fetched). MSI and EBV status are absent from all GEO sample metadata seen.
- Licence: GEO data carry no explicit licence; PMIDs and any data-use statements in the papers not checked. GSE270680 and GSE183904 state raw reads withheld, so only processed counts are public.
- Dataset search was by eutils title query (31 hits for human gastric single-cell series); studies not on GEO (EGA, GSA, Zenodo, Synapse) were not searched.
