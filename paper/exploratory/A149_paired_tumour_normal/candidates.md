# A149 candidate cohorts: paired tumour + adjacent-normal scRNA-seq (public, no access request)

Compiled 2026-10-28 by a research subagent. Metadata only was read (GEO SOFT family files, GEO FTP `suppl/` listings, CELLxGENE collection index, Zenodo record JSON); no count matrix was downloaded or executed.
"Paired patients" = patients with at least one tumour GSM and one adjacent-normal GSM, counted from GSM titles / characteristics (verified) unless flagged "unverified".
Already-in-hand accessions in the task brief were excluded. Sizes are the GEO `suppl/` sizes (RAW.tar = tar of per-sample files).
Treatment status is as stated in GEO/paper text; "not stated" means nothing found in the GEO record (paper not checked).
