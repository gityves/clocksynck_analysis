## 3. clocksync capabilities and gaps for the Q2 simulation (`clocksync/src/clocksync`)
Public API: `Dataset` (`gate`, `fit`, `contrast`, `coherence`, `agreement`, `timed`, `timed_loadings`, `gene_rhythms`, `joint`, `own_rhythm`, `lower_bound`, `design_bias`, `gate_power`, `host`, `stability`), `paired` (`paired`, `cross`, `ccorr`, `swap_null`, `reference_gate`, `pseudo_waveforms`), `decompose`, `cells.vonmises` (`group_moments`, `group_stats`, `solve`, `model`, `simulate`), `cells.dispersion` (`dispersion`, `dispersion_null`, `pooled_with_se`), `power` (`mde`, `gate_power`, `damped`), `simulate` (`simulate`, `Compartment`, `like`, `thin_clock`, `clock_change`, `ambient`), `stats` (`meta`, `bh`), `waveform` (`gtex`, `fit_waveform`).
- `simulate.simulate(waveform, compartments, n, window_h=(8,18), jitter_h, amplitude, depth, tau, shift_sd, shared_sd, seed)`: pseudobulk-level counts; `Compartment(z, psi, coupled, mesor_sd, presence, gene_shift_h, tau)`.
  - Partial-day windows: yes (`window_h`, `jitter_h`). Amplitude loss: yes (`z` modulus, `amplitude`). Patient-specific phase and amplitude: partly, as isotropic clock-plane noise `psi` per resection (random amplitude and phase) or `coupled=False` (uniform phase independent of host); no explicit patient-specific phase-offset distribution (e.g. von Mises around the host), no patient-specific gene-phase relations (`gene_shift_h` is per compartment, not per patient). Shared non-clock program: `shared_sd`.
  - Genes: only the waveform's genes (14 clock genes); no genome-wide background, so it cannot feed COFE genome-wide or CYCLOPS (which need hundreds to thousands of genes and use non-clock structure as seen in the benchmark).
  - Per-cell data: `cells.vonmises.simulate(n_groups, n_cells, c, p, lam, kappa, depth_mean, mus)` gives cells per group with von Mises phases around a group mean (kappa = within-patient synchrony, lam amplitude), 14 genes; no patient-specific offset beyond `mus`, no clones.
  - `Dataset.like` / `simulate.like`: simulator at the real design (hosts, noise, strata) of one compartment, drawn at a chosen true coupling.
- Gaps for Q2: (i) genome-wide simulator with a clock module and non-clock programs (proliferation, copy number, purity mixing) at tumour-like heterogeneity, calibrated to TCGA or sc data (WP0 `reconcile_calibrated.py` calibration, B / H / sd_off, is a template but 14-gene); (ii) sampling-time model with arbitrary coverage (U on a window; exists) and patient-specific phase offsets; (iii) per-cell simulator with patient hierarchy for Q1; (iv) wrappers returning ordering error vs known truth for CoFE and CYCLOPS (scoring is in `common/benchmark/evaluate.py`, paper-only; not in clocksync). Per CLAUDE.md, a generic `simulate` extension (patient-specific offset distribution, background genes) would be public-API material.
- Power / MDE: `power.mde`, `power.gate_power`, `Dataset.lower_bound`, `Dataset.design_bias` exist (pseudobulk designs, coupling).

## 5. Risks
1. **Cohort size.** Single-cell cohorts have 6-60 patients (median 10 over 36 cohorts in the gate table); only 21 pass the gate, 13 with CNV barcodes; paired malignant + normal epithelium + host only 79 patients in 12 studies, 10 patients or fewer per cohort for many. Per-cohort variance components will have wide intervals (cf. D134.3 +-10, A97 H97.1 [-4.8, 2.2]). Pooling across cohorts adds a study component.
2. **Whole-transcriptome per-cell data are not stored.** Only 14 clock genes plus epithelial program scores exist for most cohorts; CoFE and CYCLOPS tests on single cells or on genome-wide pseudobulks need rebuilding from raw h5ad (6 cohorts in `$D/c1_cells`, others from scBaseCount / GEO).
3. **Coverage unknown.** No collection times anywhere for tumours; TCGA samples are surgical (hospital hours). The GTEx window result (08-18 h) is the only calibration; sc cohort windows can only be inferred (the A25 / `d1_sigma_axis` window estimates: axial R 0.72, mean about 10.8 h). Placing TCGA on the simulation map needs a coverage assumption stated as such.
4. **Sparse Poisson floor.** Median clock UMI per cell 1.2-4.0 depending on compartment (`a18_cancer_split.csv`; malignant 2.86, fibroblast 3.98); immune compartments sit at the null. Per-cell variance partitions are noise-dominated; need the Poisson-corrected estimator and a simulated recovery check at the real depth.
5. **Simulator calibration.** WP0 calibration reproduced normal epithelium (share, coherence, alignment, D) but not coupling (0.114 vs 0.325) or lambda (2.07 vs 3.18); no scenario fits pseudobulk readouts and D together. Any simulation for Q2 inherits this open reconciliation; a failed calibration is not evidence of decoupling.
6. **Compute.** COFE genome-wide at n = 80 about 28 min, TCGA-sized runs 11-42 min per cancer at 16 workers; CYCLOPS about 31 min at n = 80 with 8 workers; the budget is 8 workers in total, so a simulation grid with many replicates (e.g. 100 designs x 20 reps x 2 methods) is days; use clock-gene-plus-background subsets (COFE clock-gene runs take about 72 s) and fewer genes, and run via `run.sh`. CYCLOPS needs Julia 1.6.7 and fixes its internal RNG seed; replicates must shuffle sample order. COFE has no per-sample ordering truth in TCGA, so only internal diagnostics can be compared there.
7. **Validity flags.** COFE's interior CV minimum fires in every run, window included (arch RP line 508); the benchmark failure rates (0.19-0.24 at n = 80 on GTEx) are the only reference. The "arrangement matches truth" check does not validate orderings (anchoring breast case).
8. **Q3 design.** TCGA tumour-normal pairs (10 cancers, 19-112 pairs; OV none) are bulk: tumour clock-gene signal mixes stroma, immune and malignant cells (purity companion in A97 goes the opposite way: stroma-rich more coherent). The between-patient "follows own normal" contrast needs the normal's own state estimated without time; the shared sampling window across patients narrows the phase spread (A84 cue tests uninformative for that reason). Sign convention: negative offset = delayed (A54 note).
9. **Data gaps to confirm before registering.** `$D/cells/cancer/*_dispersion.csv.gz` and `$D/pseudobulk/cancer_sc*.parquet` are not on disk (archive fallback used); barcodes exist only for the 13 cohorts of the cancer split (the rest need `cells_cancer/build.py`); stored locus-level copy number only for the 79 A134 patients. Registered A138 text does not exist yet in `paper/docs/registered_experiments.md` (last entry A135 note / A134).
