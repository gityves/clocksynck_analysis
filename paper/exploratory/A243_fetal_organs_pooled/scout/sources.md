# A243 scout: open human fetal heart, kidney, lung, liver data (metadata only, 2026-11-15)

Metadata only: no expression matrix downloaded, no clock statistic computed. Small metadata files (series matrices,
SDRFs, ENA/ENCODE/CELLxGENE/HCA JSON) were parsed from `/tmp/.../scratchpad/a243_meta/` with `python -I`. Rules E1-E6 as
written in A243. E4 (median clock-gene count >= 20) cannot be checked without data: "E4 to check" everywhere.
"Fetuses" follow the A243 rule (same stated age in one source = one fetus unless the source names different
individuals or sexes differ). Verdict codes: **OK** = passes E1-E3, E5, E6 on metadata (E4 pending); **COND** = needs a
decision by the caller (reason given); **EXCL(Ex)** = excluded by rule x. Age convention is quoted as the source states it;
"not stated" means the page gives none.

Key uncertainties (details in the rows): (a) ENCODE UW polyA fetuses vs Cao 2020 donors (same biobank programme; two
same-age/same-sex coincidences, see E6 notes); (b) He/Barnes 2022-23 sample sheets do not state cell enrichment;
(c) Quach's counts sit only inside an 8 GB Seurat `.rds` (not verified to hold raw counts); (d) age conventions are
mixed (post conception / post fertilisation / gestational / unstated); (e) Han HCL liver Liver1-1 and Liver1-2 differ by
one week (26 vs 27 wk) but share a donor-style name; (f) Roost heart samples are single chambers for four of six fetuses.

## Cross-checks and things the caller should know

1. **A242 cohorts vs these organs.** Roost 2015 (GSE66302) covers all four organs (6 fetuses each; heart 6 fetuses but
   9 samples because atrium and ventricle are separate); the Han HCL series covers heart 2, kidney 4, lung 2, liver 1-2.
   None of the other A242 adrenal series (Kameneva, Dong, Wang, Sasaki, Schroder, Kildisiute) holds these organs.
2. **E6 candidates.** (a) ENCODE UW polyA vs Cao: heart 120 d M (ENCSR047LLJ) vs Cao H27098 (120, M); kidney 85 d F
   (ENCSR896QPD) vs Cao H27798 (85, F). Same stated age and sex; ENCODE gives "day" and Cao `ga_days`, conventions not
   verified; A242 recorded the ENCODE adrenal as distinct from Cao and kept the same reading. (b) Stewart fetal kidney
   (F16, F17, F35) = Popescu kidney (F4, F6, F3) by library names; count once. (c) Suo 2022 repeats Popescu and
   Stewart samples. (d) UK HDBR donors (Cardoso-Moreira, He, Barnes, Popescu, Stewart, Knight-Schrijver) cannot be
   cross-matched on metadata (no shared donor identifiers other than within the Teichmann/HCA sources).
3. **Download sizes.** All OK items together: heart 0.67 GB, kidney 0.26 GB, lung 10.5 GB (Quach 7.98 GB of it), liver
   0.02 GB. Adding every COND source: kidney +0.85 GB, lung +3.5 GB (Barnes), liver +1.7 GB. Far below the 60 GB cap.
4. **Counts exactness.** Exact counts: GEO cellranger matrices (Hochane, Sountoulidis, Knight-Schrijver), UMI tables
   (Cui, Renesme, Han dge), ENCODE gene quantification files (expected counts), cellxgene h5ad (raw counts required by
   the schema, to be verified on first non-zeros as for gonad2022), Roost (raw reads table). Quach: only a Seurat object.
5. **Unverified items** (not checked on a source page): author/lab for GSE195911, GSE156703, GSE167096, Wang 2018 as
   named; Roadmap Epigenomics RNA-seq (not opened); Suryawanshi, Hill, Miao, Sahara (no open accession returned by
   search); whether He 2022 / Barnes 2023 libraries were sorted; Quach's tissue source; whether Cui's samples are
   one-heart-per-name group; CSHL and UW ENCODE age conventions; cell versus nuclei in GSE280880.
6. **Controlled-access sources, excluded by E2, not requested:** Asp 2019 (EGA EGAS00001003996), Farah 2024 raw (dbGaP
   phs002031), Haniffa kidney tumour set (EGA), Sountoulidis raw reads (EGA; processed counts are open on GEO).
